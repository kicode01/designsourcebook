# Audi UI

## Overview

**Audi UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#2d3232` primary ink (13.0:1 contrast) and `#87db87` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Cranny` (48px display down to 20px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Audi**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://styleguide.audi.com/document/1811#/-/colours). Inventory: **91** colors, **118** typography steps, and **40** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#2d3232` — body copy and primary headings (13.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#2d3232` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#87db87` (1.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 11.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #2d3232 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `rc-focus-color` (`#08f`), `color-charts-secondary-default` (`#d59bff`), `fc-primary-hover-color` (`#87db87`), `fc-primary-base-color` (`#6acb75`), `fc-secondary-hover-color` (`#e5b3ff`), `box-selected-color-pressed` (`#c8d1ed`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#2d3232` primary text and `#2d3232` secondary labels.
- Use `#87db87` with `#0b0b0c` text (11.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Cranny` headings with `Cranny` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #2d3232 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
