# eBay Skin

## Overview

**eBay Skin** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#e20020` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Market Sans` (64px display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f7f7f7` surface layering.

Maintained by **eBay**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/eBay/evo-web), repo https://github.com/eBay/evo-web, package `@ebay/skin`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **20** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f7f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#e20020` (4.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-brand-tise-dark-purple` (`#422136`), `color-brand-tise-peach` (`#ff8f77`), `expressive-theme-ag-indigo-dark-background` (`#01193d`), `expressive-theme-ag-indigo-light-background` (`#d3effe`), `expressive-theme-avocado-dark-background` (`#4e4e0c`), `expressive-theme-avocado-light-background` (`#e3f13c`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-brand-tise-tan` (`#ffefeb`), `color-neutral-100` (`#ffffff`), `color-neutral-200` (`#f7f7f7`), `color-neutral-300` (`#e5e5e5`), `color-neutral-400` (`#c7c7c7`), `color-neutral-500` (`#8f8f8f`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `expressive-theme-green-dark-background` (`#345110`) · danger: `color-brand-depop-red` (`#e20020`) · warning: `expressive-theme-orange-dark-background` (`#562501`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Market Sans"` (top step `font-size-64`: 4rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Market Sans"` (`font-size-body`: 0.875rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (20)**: `font-size-64` (4rem), `font-size-giant-3` (2.875rem), `font-size-46` (2.875rem), `font-size-giant-2` (2.25rem), `font-size-36` (2.25rem), `font-size-giant-1` (1.875rem), `font-size-30` (1.875rem), `font-size-large-2` (1.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f7f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f7f7f7`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#e20020` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#e20020` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Market Sans` headings with `Market Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f7f7` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
