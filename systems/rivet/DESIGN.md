# Rivet Design System

## Overview

**Rivet Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#006298` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `BentonSans` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `0.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Indiana University**. Source: published token package (https://rivet.iu.edu), package `rivet-core`. Inventory: **91** colors, **26** typography steps, and **37** documented components (BSD-3-Clause license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#FFFCEB` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#006298` (6.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 6.1:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `300` (`#75838F`), `500` (`#243142`), `color-black-400` (`#4C5A69`), `color-black-600` (`#182534`), `color-black-base` (`#243142`), `color-blue-100` (`#C6ECF6`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `100` (`#E2E7E9`), `700` (`#0E1825`), `color-black-200` (`#A0ABB4`), `color-blue-000` (`#EDFAFD`), `color-gold-000` (`#FFFCEB`), `color-orange-000` (`#FFF3F0`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-green-200` (`#A7D094`) · warning: `color-orange-100` (`#FFCDC0`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `BentonSans, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Open Sans", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 2rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `BentonSans, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Open Sans", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (`font-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `monospace` (`font-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (26)**: `h1` (2rem), `rvt-hero-title` (2rem), `rvt-billboard-title` (1.8125rem), `h2` (1.4375rem), `rvt-timeline-heading` (1.4375rem, w400), `h3` (1.25rem), `rvt-card-title` (1.25rem), `rvt-dialog-title` (1.25rem, w700).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 37 component tokens with documented paddings: `1rem`, `0.1875rem 0.5rem`, `0.5rem`, `0 0.625rem`, `0 0.75rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#FFFCEB` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0.25rem 1rem rgba(36, 49, 66, 0.2)`, `0 0.25rem 1rem rgba(36, 49, 66, 0.2)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#006298` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0.25rem` (Buttons `0.25rem` · Inputs `0.25rem` · Badges `999rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `0.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#006298` with `#f7f7f8` text (6.1:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `BentonSans` headings with `BentonSans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `0.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (0.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#FFFCEB` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `0.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
