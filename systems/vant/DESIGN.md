# Vant

## Overview

**Vant** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff976a` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (160px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Youzan**. Source: published token package, package `vant`. Inventory: **28** colors, **14** typography steps, and **38** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f8fa` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff976a` (2.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `van-blue` (`#1989fa`), `primary` (`#ff976a`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `van-black` (`#000`), `van-white` (`#fff`), `van-gray-1` (`#f7f8fa`), `van-gray-2` (`#f2f3f5`), `van-gray-3` (`#ebedf0`), `van-gray-4` (`#dcdee0`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `van-green` (`#07c160`) · danger: `van-red` (`#ee0a24`) · warning: `van-orange` (`#ff976a`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 38 component tokens with documented paddings: `0 4px`, `8px 16px`, `0 3px`, `16px`, `0 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f8fa` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 3px 1px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, .05)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff976a` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff976a` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f8fa` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `van-password-input` fields and secondary buttons.
