# Cauldron

## Overview

**Cauldron** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f0f2f5` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (17.5:1 contrast) and `#3c7aae` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Roboto` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `3px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Deque**. Source: published token package, package `@deque/cauldron-styles`. Inventory: **91** colors, **2** typography steps, and **29** documented components (MPL-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f0f2f5` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (17.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#3c7aae` (4.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#b3bfc6` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `accent-primary` (`#3c7aae`), `accent-primary-active` (`#316091`), `accent-primary-disabled` (`#78a6d8`), `focus-light` (`#b51ad1`), `focus-dark` (`#f5a4ff`), `metric-accent-mid` (`#8ba6c5`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `accent-dark` (`#02101a`), `accent-medium` (`#283640`), `accent-light` (`#d4dde0`), `background-light` (`#f0f2f5`), `workspace-background-color` (`#f0f2f5`), `field-listbox-selected-text-color` (`#fff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `accent-success` (`#a5db75`) · danger: `accent-error` (`#d93251`) · warning: `accent-warning` (`#ffdd75`) · info: `accent-info` (`#6cdaf2`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `badge-font-size`: 1rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`base-font-family`: 16px, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 29 component tokens with documented paddings: `16px`, `0 8px`, `8px 16px`, `0 16px 0 12px`, `0 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#b3bfc6` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 2px 4px -2px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`, `0 8px 8px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 20px 24px -4px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.08)`) paired with `#b3bfc6` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#3c7aae` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `3px` (Buttons `3px` · Badges `2.25rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `3px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f0f2f5` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#3c7aae` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Roboto` headings with `Roboto` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `3px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (3px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `#b3bfc6` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `3px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
