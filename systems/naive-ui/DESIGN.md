# Naive UI

## Overview

**Naive UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFF` canvas surfaces with `rgb(31, 34, 37)` primary ink (16.0:1 contrast) and `#7fe7c4` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `v-sans` (48px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **TuSimple**. Source: published token package (https://www.naiveui.com), package `naive-ui`. Inventory: **91** colors, **46** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `rgb(31, 34, 37)` — body copy and primary headings (16.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `rgb(51, 54, 57)` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#7fe7c4` (1.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 13.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `rgb(224, 224, 230)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primary-hover` (`#7fe7c4`), `primary-default` (`#63e2b7`), `primary-active` (`#5acea7`), `primary-suppl` (`rgb(42, 148, 125)`), `antiquewhite` (`#FAEBD7`), `blueviolet` (`#8A2BE2`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `background-color-default` (`#FFF`), `icon-background-color-default` (`#FFF`), `neutral-base` (`#000`), `neutral-invert-base` (`#fff`), `neutral-text-base` (`#fff`), `neutral-popover` (`rgb(72, 72, 78)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success-hover` (`#7fe7c4`) · danger: `error-hover` (`#e98b8b`) · warning: `warning-hover` (`#f5d599`) · info: `info-hover` (`#8acbec`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `rgb(224, 224, 230)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#FFF` -> `#fff`) and `1px solid rgb(224, 224, 230)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#7fe7c4` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFF` (light) with `rgb(31, 34, 37)` primary text and `rgb(51, 54, 57)` secondary labels.
- Use `#7fe7c4` with `#0b0b0c` text (13.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `v-sans` headings with `v-sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `rgb(224, 224, 230)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
