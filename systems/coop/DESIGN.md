# Co-op Experience Library

## Overview

**Co-op Experience Library** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f5f5f0` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.0:1 contrast) and `#00b1e7` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Avenir-Next` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#f5f5f0` -> `#f3f3f3` surface layering.

Maintained by **Co-op**. Source: published token package, package `@coopdigital/foundations-vars`. Inventory: **91** colors, **1** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f5f5f0` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#00b1e7` with `#0b0b0c` text (7.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Avenir-Next` headings with `Avenir-Next` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f3f3f3` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
