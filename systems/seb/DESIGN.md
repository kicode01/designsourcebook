# SEB Green Design System

## Overview

**SEB Green Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#333` primary ink (12.6:1 contrast) and `#007ac7` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `SEB SansSerif` (82px display down to 20px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#eeeeee` surface layering.

Maintained by **Skandinaviska Enskilda Banken**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/seb-oss/green), repo https://github.com/seb-oss/green. Inventory: **90** colors, **52** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#eeeeee` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#333` — body copy and primary headings (12.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#333` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#007ac7` (4.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #333 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `6` (`#CCE7FF`), `11` (`#4DACFF`), `16` (`#006DCC`), `21` (`#00294D`), `26` (`#003D26`), `gds-comp-check-mark-background` (`#60cd18`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `1` (`#FAFDFF`), `gds-sys-color-text-primary` (`#333333`), `gds-sys-color-text-secondary` (`#ababab`), `gds-sys-color-text-white` (`#ffffff`), `gds-sys-color-text-black` (`#333333`), `gds-sys-color-text-inverted` (`#ffffff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green-s100` (`#8fd668`) · danger: `gds-sys-color-text-error` (`#9f000a`) · warning: `gds-sys-color-yellow` (`#ffc500`) · info: `gds-sys-color-text-link` (`#0062bc`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"SEB SansSerif", "SEB SansSerif GDS", SEBSansSerif, sans-serif, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial` (top step `display-2xl`: 82px, weight 600, line-height 90).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"SEB SansSerif", "SEB SansSerif GDS", SEBSansSerif, sans-serif, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial` (`body-regular-l`: 20px, weight 400, line-height 26).
- **Scale Steps (52)**: `display-2xl` (82px), `display-xl` (64px), `display-l` (48px), `bb-chart-arcs-gauge-title` (2.7em), `display-m` (36px), `heading-xl` (34px), `display-s` (32px), `preamble-2xl` (32px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#eeeeee` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #333 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#eeeeee`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #333 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#007ac7` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Components

- **`select-default` (select)**: bg `#eeeeee`, ink `#333`, (10.9:1), radius `8px`, padding `8px 12px`.
- **`switch-control` (switch)**: bg `#007ac7`, ink `#000000`, (4.6:1), radius `999px`, padding `2px`.
- **`checkbox-control` (checkbox)**: bg `#007ac7`, ink `#000000`, (4.6:1), radius `4px`, padding `2px`.
- **`tabs-nav` (tabs)**: bg `#eeeeee`, ink `#333`, (10.9:1), radius `8px`, padding `6px 12px`.
- **`alert-info` (alert)**: bg `#eeeeee`, ink `#333`, (10.9:1), radius `8px`, padding `12px 14px`.
- **`table-grid` (table)**: bg `#ffffff`, ink `#333`, (12.6:1), radius `8px`, padding `10px 14px`.
- **`tooltip-overlay` (tooltip)**: bg `#333`, ink `#ffffff`, (12.6:1), radius `6px`, padding `6px 10px`.
- **`icon-glyph` (icon)**: bg `#eeeeee`, ink `#333`, (10.9:1), radius `8px`, padding `8px`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#333` primary text and `#333` secondary labels.
- Use `#007ac7` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `SEB SansSerif` headings with `SEB SansSerif` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#eeeeee` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #333 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
