# Salt Design System

## Overview

**Salt Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `rgb(255, 255, 255)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `rgb(255, 167, 156)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (56px display down to 18px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `rgb(255, 255, 255)` -> `rgb(245, 247, 248)` surface layering.

Maintained by **J.P. Morgan**. Source: published token package, package `@salt-ds/theme`. Inventory: **91** colors, **48** typography steps, and **8** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `rgb(255, 255, 255)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgb(245, 247, 248)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `rgb(255, 167, 156)` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `salt-color-blue-200-rgb` (`rgb(199, 222, 255)`), `salt-color-blue-800-rgb` (`rgb(0, 45, 89)`), `salt-color-brown-500-rgb` (`rgb(153, 108, 72)`), `salt-color-teal-500-rgb` (`rgb(27, 127, 158)`), `salt-color-purple-200-rgb` (`rgb(240, 214, 245)`), `salt-color-purple-800-rgb` (`rgb(73, 21, 82)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `salt-color-background-snow-rgb` (`rgb(255, 255, 255)`), `salt-color-background-marble-rgb` (`rgb(245, 247, 248)`), `salt-color-background-limestone-rgb` (`rgb(250, 248, 242)`), `salt-color-background-titanium-rgb` (`rgb(226, 228, 229)`), `salt-color-background-jet-rgb` (`rgb(16, 24, 32)`), `salt-color-background-granite-rgb` (`rgb(26, 34, 41)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `salt-color-green-200-rgb` (`rgb(184, 229, 209)`) · danger: `salt-color-red-500-rgb` (`rgb(229, 33, 53)`) · warning: `salt-color-orange-200-rgb` (`rgb(255, 198, 161)`).

- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Open Sans"` (`salt-text-body2-font-size`: 18px, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgb(245, 247, 248)` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`rgb(255, 255, 255)` -> `rgb(245, 247, 248)`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `rgb(255, 167, 156)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `rgb(255, 255, 255)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `rgb(255, 167, 156)` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgb(245, 247, 248)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
