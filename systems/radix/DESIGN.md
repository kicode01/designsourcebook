# Radix

## Overview

**Radix** is a dark-first design system anchored on `#111111` canvas surfaces with `#f7f7f8` primary ink (17.6:1 contrast) and `#ffc53d` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `6px` base corner radius and stepped `#111111` -> `#141110` surface layering.

Maintained by **WorkOS**. Source: published token package, repo https://github.com/radix-ui/primitives, package `@radix-ui/colors`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **4** typography steps, and **16** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#111111` — base application backdrop (dark theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#141110` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#f7f7f8` — body copy and primary headings (17.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#f7f7f8` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ffc53d` (12.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 12.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `blue-5` (`#004074`), `bronze-9` (`#a18072`), `crimson-9` (`#e93d82`), `cyan-5` (`#004558`), `gold-9` (`#978365`), `grass-5` (`#25482d`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `amber-1` (`#16120c`), `bronze-1` (`#141110`), `brown-5` (`#3e3128`), `crimson-1` (`#191114`), `gold-1` (`#121211`), `gray-1` (`#111111`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green-5` (`#174933`) · danger: `red5` (`#611623`) · warning: `amber-9` (`#ffc53d`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI (Custom)", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", "Open Sans (Custom)", system-ui, sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji"` (top step `default-font-family`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI (Custom)", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", "Open Sans (Custom)", system-ui, sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji"` (`default-font-family`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Menlo, "Consolas (Custom)", "Bitstream Vera Sans Mono", monospace, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji"` (`code-font-family`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (4)**: `default-font-family` (16px), `code-font-family` (16px), `em-font-family` (16px), `quote-font-family` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#141110` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#111111` -> `#141110`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ffc53d` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `6px` (Buttons `6px` · Inputs `6px` · Cards `10px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `6px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `10px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Radix Icons (`@radix-ui/react-icons`) on a `15x15` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `square` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#111111` (dark) with `#f7f7f8` primary text and `#f7f7f8` secondary labels.
- Use `#ffc53d` with `#0b0b0c` text (12.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `6px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (10px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#141110` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of dark polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `6px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
