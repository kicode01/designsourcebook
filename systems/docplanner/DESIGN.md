# Watson

## Overview

**Watson** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#004ee1` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f8f9fb` surface layering.

Maintained by **Docplanner**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://watson.docplanner.design/). Inventory: **91** colors, **49** typography steps, and **8** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f9fb` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#004ee1` (6.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 6.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `dm-color-outline-primary` (`#004ee1`), `dm-color-foreground-neutral-faded` (`#66728a`), `dm-color-foreground-neutral-placeholder` (`#8390a9`), `dm-color-foreground-disabled` (`#a2aec8`), `dm-color-background-primary` (`#104fc6`), `dm-color-background-upgrade` (`#7f22fe`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `dm-color-background-page` (`#ffffff`), `dm-color-background-page-faded` (`#f8f9fb`), `dm-color-background-elevation-base` (`#ffffff`), `dm-color-background-elevation-raised` (`#feffff`), `dm-color-background-elevation-overlay` (`#feffff`), `dm-color-foreground-neutral` (`#000001`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `dm-color-background-positive` (`#008236`) · danger: `dm-color-background-critical` (`#c50027`) · warning: `dm-color-background-warning` (`#d0571e`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter, sans-serif` (top step `dm-font-size-title-1`: 32px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter, sans-serif` (`dm-font-size-body-1`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Roboto Mono"` (`dm-font-family-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (49)**: `dm-font-size-title-1` (32px), `dm-font-size-featured-1` (32px), `dm-font-size-docs-title-1` (28px), `dm-font-size-title-2` (24px), `dm-font-size-featured-2` (24px), `dm-font-size-docs-title-2` (24px), `dm-font-size-title-3` (20px), `dm-font-size-featured-3` (20px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f9fb` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f8f9fb`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#004ee1` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#004ee1` with `#f7f7f8` text (6.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f9fb` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
