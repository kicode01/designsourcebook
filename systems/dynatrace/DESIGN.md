# Strato Design System

## Overview

**Strato Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#e01032` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (large display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `#fafafb` surface layering.

Maintained by **Dynatrace**. Source: published token package (https://developer.dynatrace.com/design/design-tokens/), package `@dynatrace/strato-design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **0** typography steps, and **8** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fafafb` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#e01032` (4.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#eaeaed` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `dt-colors-theme-primary-50` (`#c3d8ff`), `dt-colors-theme-primary-90` (`#0042da`), `background-field-primary-emphasized-hover` (`#d1e1fd`), `rs-background-field-primary-accent-active` (`#0042da`), `dt-colors-border-primary-default-hover` (`#c3d8ff`), `dt-colors-border-primary-accent-active` (`#0017ae`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#e01032` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fafafb` surface shifts and `#eaeaed` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
