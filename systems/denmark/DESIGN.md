# Det Fælles Designsystem

## Overview

**Det Fælles Designsystem** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFFFFF` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0059B3` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `IBM Plex Sans` (45px display down to 10px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Denmark**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://designsystem.dk/). Inventory: **15** colors, **21** typography steps, and **29** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFFFFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F5F5F5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0059B3` (6.8:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 6.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-primary` (`#0059B3`), `primary` (`#0059B3`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `alert-background-color` (`#DADAEB`), `color-white` (`#FFFFFF`), `color-gray-100` (`#F5F5F5`), `color-gray-200` (`#DCDCDC`), `color-gray-300` (`#BFBFBF`), `color-gray-400` (`#8E8E8E`).
- **Semantic States**: danger: `alert-border-color` (`#6A51A3`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"IBM Plex Sans", system-ui, system, sans-serif` (top step `fds-title`: 2.8rem, weight 600, line-height 3.2rem).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"IBM Plex Sans", system-ui, system, sans-serif` (`html`: 62.5%, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (21)**: `fds-title` (2.8rem, w600), `displayheading-1` (2.4rem, w500), `card-heading` (2.4rem, w600), `displayheading-2` (2.1rem, w500), `menu-heading` (2rem), `solution-heading` (2rem, w600), `top-heading` (2rem), `font-lead` (2rem, w400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 29 component tokens with documented paddings: `0`, `1.6rem`, `16px`, `1px`, `1.6rem 1.6rem 1.6rem 5.6rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F5F5F5` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 8px 24px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.2)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0059B3` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Inputs `8px` · Badges `1.2rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFFFFF` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0059B3` with `#f7f7f8` text (6.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `IBM Plex Sans` headings with `IBM Plex Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F5F5F5` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
