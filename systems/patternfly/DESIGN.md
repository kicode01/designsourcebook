# PatternFly

## Overview

**PatternFly** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0066cc` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `redhatdisplay` with `redhattext` body copy (36px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f2f2f2` surface layering.

Maintained by **Red Hat**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/patternfly/patternfly-react#readme), repo https://github.com/patternfly/patternfly-react, package `@patternfly/react-tokens`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **49** typography steps, and **16** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f2f2f2` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0066cc` (5.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 5.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e0e0e0` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `t-global-background-color-highlight-100` (`#ffcc17`), `t-global-border-color-brand-subtle-clicked` (`#0066cc`), `t-global-border-color-hover` (`#004d99`), `t-global-border-color-status-custom-clicked` (`#004d4d`), `t-global-color-brand-500` (`#032142`), `t-global-color-brand-hover` (`#003366`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `t-global-background-color-300` (`#e0e0e0`), `t-global-border-color-300` (`#8c8c8c`), `t-global-color-disabled-300` (`#707070`), `t-global-icon-color-disabled` (`#a3a3a3`), `t-global-icon-color-on-disabled` (`#707070`), `t-global-icon-color-status-on-info-clicked` (`#ffffff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `t-global-border-color-nonstatus-green-hover` (`#204d00`) · danger: `v6-chart-global-danger-color-100` (`#b1380b`) · warning: `t-global-color-status-warning-hover` (`#54330b`) · info: `t-global-color-status-info-clicked` (`#21134d`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f2f2f2` cards; separate sibling sections using `#e0e0e0` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f2f2f2`) and `1px solid #e0e0e0` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0066cc` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `4px` · Inputs `4px` · Cards `8px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `4px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: PatternFly Icons (`@patternfly/react-icons`) on a `16x16` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0066cc` with `#f7f7f8` text (5.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `redhatdisplay` headings with `redhattext` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f2f2f2` surface shifts and `#e0e0e0` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
