# Biings Design System

## Overview

**Biings Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#8c50b9` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Plush` with `Source Sans Pro` body copy (40px display down to 15px body), unified by `7px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Biings**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://biings.design/#/color). Inventory: **72** colors, **7** typography steps, and **24** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7efe9` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#8c50b9` (5.3:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-secondary` (`#cb9569`), `color-secondary-dark` (`#a27754`), `color-secondary-light` (`#dbb596`), `color-cyan` (`#01bfea`), `color-cyan-dark` (`#018fb0`), `color-cyan-light` (`#4dd2f0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-secondary-lighter` (`#f7efe9`), `color-purple-lighter` (`#eedcf0`), `color-pink-lighter` (`#f9e2ed`), `color-green-lighter` (`#e5efde`), `color-black` (`#130a1a`), `color-black-bis` (`#241b27`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-green` (`#7db058`) · danger: `color-red` (`#f45564`) · warning: `color-orange` (`#f77d2c`) · info: `color-info` (`#2973cc`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Plush, serif` (top step `h1`: 2.5rem, weight 300).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Source Sans Pro", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` (`body`: 15px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `h1` (2.5rem, w300), `title` (1.75rem, w400), `modal-card-title` (1.5rem), `subtitle` (1.25rem, w400), `html` (16px), `body` (15px), `heading` (11px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 24 component tokens with documented paddings: `.25em .875em`, `1.65rem 1.75rem`, `1.625rem 1.75rem`, `.8em`, `8px 12px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7efe9` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(19,10,26,.06)`, `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(19,10,26,.06)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#8c50b9` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `7px` (Buttons `7px` · Inputs `7px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `7px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#8c50b9` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Plush` headings with `Source Sans Pro` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `7px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (7px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7efe9` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `7px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
