# Protocol

## Overview

**Protocol** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#cb9eff` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Mozilla Headline` with `Inter` body copy (56px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Mozilla**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/mozilla/protocol), repo https://github.com/mozilla/protocol, package `@mozilla-protocol/core`, frameworks: css. Inventory: **91** colors, **32** typography steps, and **16** documented components (MPL-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f9f9fb` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#cb9eff` (2.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-blue-05` (`#aaf2ff`), `color-blue-10` (`#80ebff`), `color-blue-20` (`#00ddff`), `color-blue-30` (`#00b3f4`), `color-blue-40` (`#0090ed`), `color-blue-50` (`#0060df`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-light-gray-05` (`#ffffff`), `color-light-gray-10` (`#f9f9fb`), `color-light-gray-20` (`#f0f0f4`), `color-light-gray-30` (`#e0e0e6`), `color-light-gray-40` (`#cfcfd8`), `color-light-gray-50` (`#bfbfc9`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-green-05` (`#e3fff3`) · danger: `color-red-05` (`#ffdfe7`) · warning: `color-orange-05` (`#fff4de`) · info: `color-link` (`#0060df`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Mozilla Headline", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, X-LocaleSpecific, sans-serif` (top step `mzp-u-title-2xl`: 56px, weight 600, line-height 1.143).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter, X-LocaleSpecific, sans-serif` (`font-stack-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Consolas, Monaco` (`font-stack-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (32)**: `mzp-u-title-2xl` (56px), `mzp-u-title-xl` (48px), `mzp-c-article-title` (40px), `h1` (40px), `mzp-u-title-lg` (40px), `mzp-c-billboard-title` (32px), `mzp-c-newsletter-title` (32px), `mzp-c-section-heading` (32px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 16px`, `32px 4px`, `0 32px 0 6px`, `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f9f9fb` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 16px 16px -16px rgba(0,0,0,.3)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#cb9eff` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Inputs `4px` · Badges `999px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#cb9eff` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Mozilla Headline` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f9f9fb` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
