# Pivotal

## Overview

**Pivotal** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0070ec` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Source Sans Pro` (42px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` surface layering.

Source: published token package (https://github.com/pivotal-cf/pivotal-ui), repo https://github.com/pivotal-cf/pivotal-ui, package `pivotal-ui`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **82** colors, **28** typography steps, and **16** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0070ec` (4.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e5e5e5` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `dark-gray` (`#253640`), `gray` (`#647882`), `gray-on-lite` (`#5C6D76`), `light-blue` (`#c2e7ff`), `accent-blue` (`#1a98ff`), `blue` (`#0070ec`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `black` (`#000000`), `gray-on-dark` (`#919FA8`), `accent-gray` (`#89969f`), `light-gray` (`#eaedef`), `white` (`#ffffff`), `glow-5` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `light-green` (`#b9ecac`) · danger: `light-red` (`#fed9db`) · warning: `light-yellow` (`#fff0bc`) · info: `state-info-text` (`#8e8837`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Source Sans Pro", SourceSansPro, sans-serif` (top step `font-size-title`: 42px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Source Sans Pro", SourceSansPro, sans-serif` (`font-size-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Source Code Pro", SourceCodePro, monospace` (`font-family-monospace`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (28)**: `font-size-title` (42px), `title` (42px), `font-size-h1` (32px), `h1` (2em), `font-size-h2` (24px), `font-size-extra-large` (20px), `font-size-h3` (20px), `carousel-control-font-size` (20px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` cards; separate sibling sections using `#e5e5e5` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)`) and `1px solid #e5e5e5` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0070ec` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0070ec` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Source Sans Pro` headings with `Source Sans Pro` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` surface shifts and `#e5e5e5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
