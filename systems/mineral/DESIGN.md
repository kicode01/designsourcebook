# Mineral UI

## Overview

**Mineral UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#accbfc` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (36px display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **CA Technologies**. Source: published token package (http://mineral-ui.com), repo https://github.com/mineral-ui/mineral-ui, package `mineral-ui`, frameworks: react. Inventory: **91** colors, **7** typography steps, and **8** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#accbfc` (1.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 11.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#c8d1e0` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `success-background-color-success-focus` (`#e1faeb`), `color-disabled` (`#afbacc`), `background-color-success-hover` (`#e1faeb`), `background-color-brand-selected-active` (`#accbfc`), `background-color-brand-primary` (`#3272d9`), `background-color-brand-primary-focus` (`#3272d9`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `danger-background-color-danger-hover` (`#faf0f0`), `danger-color-danger-primary` (`#ffffff`), `gray-background-color-disabled` (`#ebeff5`), `gray-border-color` (`#c8d1e0`), `gray-input-background-color-disabled` (`#ebeff5`), `gray-panel-border-color` (`#ebeff5`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success-background-color-success-primary` (`#2a854e`) · danger: `danger-background-color-danger-active` (`#fad4d4`) · warning: `warning-background-color-warning-active` (`#fad8af`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Open Sans", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (top step `h1-display`: 36px, weight 700, line-height 1.25).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Open Sans", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (`body`: 14px, weight 400, line-height 1.5).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace` (`mono`: 13px).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `h1-display` (36px, w700), `h2-heading` (28px, w600), `h3-heading` (22px, w600), `h4-subheading` (18px, w600), `body` (14px, w400), `mono` (13px, w400), `caption` (12px, w400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#c8d1e0` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid #c8d1e0` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#accbfc` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#accbfc` with `#0b0b0c` text (11.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#c8d1e0` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
