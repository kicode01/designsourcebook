# DB UI

## Overview

**DB UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#db0016` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `DB Screen Sans` (22px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Deutsche Bahn**. Source: published token package (https://db-ui.github.io/core/), package `@db-ui/core`. Inventory: **91** colors, **9** typography steps, and **17** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fdfdfd` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#db0016` (5.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `db-focus-outline-color` (`#2c95c4`), `progress-value-background-color` (`#ec0016`), `button-brand-primary-hover-background-color` (`#db0016`), `fill` (`rgb(251, 186, 0)`), `db-color-health` (`#a9455d`), `db-color-community-facilities` (`#00a099`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `form-element-border-color` (`#878c96`), `button-primary-hover-background-color` (`#2f3541`), `button-secondary-solid-hover-background-color` (`#cdd4da`), `button-secondary-solid-active-background-color` (`#c5ccd3`), `checkbox-hover-checked-background-color` (`#52575f`), `checkbox-on-dark-background-border-color` (`#fdfdfd`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `db-color-success` (`#508b1b`) · danger: `db-color-error` (`#ec0016`) · warning: `db-color-warning` (`#f75f00`) · info: `db-color-informative` (`#0087b9`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 17 component tokens with documented paddings: `1rem`, `.375rem .625rem`, `0.375rem 0.625rem`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fdfdfd` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0 5px rgba(0,0,0,.08),0 5px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.05)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#db0016` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Inputs `4px` · Cards `8px` · Badges `.25rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `20x20` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#db0016` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `DB Screen Sans` headings with `DB Screen Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fdfdfd` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
