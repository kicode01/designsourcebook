# Modus Design System

## Overview

**Modus Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f1f1f6` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (17.5:1 contrast) and `#0063a3` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (72px display down to 16px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Trimble**. Source: published token package (https://modus-bootstrap.trimble.com/), package `@trimble-oss/modus-bootstrap`. Inventory: **91** colors, **49** typography steps, and **28** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f1f1f6` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f1f1f6` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (17.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0063a3` (5.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 5.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#cbcdd6` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primary-text-dark` (`#019aeb`), `bs-primary` (`#0063a3`), `bs-primary-text-emphasis` (`#004f83`), `bs-primary-border-subtle` (`#99c1da`), `bs-primary-bg` (`#004f83`), `bs-btn-border-color` (`#0063a3`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `secondary-text-dark` (`#6a6e79`), `input-border-color-dark` (`#6a6e79`), `nav-link-disabled-color` (`#6c757d`), `pagination-focus-bg` (`#e0e1e9`), `input-focus-border-color` (`#e0e1e9`), `form-text-color` (`#464B52`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success-text-dark` (`#1e8a44`) · danger: `danger-text-dark` (`#da212c`) · warning: `warning-text-dark` (`#fbad26`) · info: `link-hover-color` (`#16517a`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 28 component tokens with documented paddings: `0.125rem 1rem`, `0.375rem 1.5rem 0.375rem 0.5rem`, `0.25rem 0.5rem`, `0.375rem 0.5rem`, `2px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f1f1f6` cards; separate sibling sections using `#cbcdd6` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f1f1f6` -> `#f1f1f6`) and `1px solid #cbcdd6` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0063a3` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `.25rem` (Inputs `.25rem` · Badges `1rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f1f1f6` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0063a3` with `#f7f7f8` text (5.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f1f1f6` surface shifts and `#cbcdd6` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-group-text` fields and secondary buttons.
