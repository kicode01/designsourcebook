# Origami v3

## Overview

**Origami v3** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff7faa` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `metric 2 VF` (84px display down to 20px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f4f4f5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Financial Times**. Source: published token package (https://registry.origami.ft.com/components/o3-foundation), package `@financial-times/o3-foundation`. Inventory: **91** colors, **60** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f4f4f5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff7faa` (2.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 8.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#cccccc` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `o3-color-use-case-page-background` (`#fff1e5`), `o3-color-use-case-button-disabled` (`#9ec0bd`), `o3-color-palette-ft-pink` (`#fcd0b1`), `o3-color-palette-claret` (`#990f3d`), `o3-color-palette-teal` (`#0d7680`), `o3-color-palette-oxford` (`#0f5499`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `o3-color-use-case-link-underline` (`#cfd8d1`), `o3-color-use-case-link-inverse-text` (`#ffffff`), `o3-color-use-case-link-inverse-text-hover` (`#d4d4d6`), `o3-color-use-case-link-inverse-underline` (`#ffffff`), `o3-color-use-case-link-inverse-underline-hover` (`#d4d4d6`), `o3-color-use-case-page-inverse-background` (`#262a33`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `o3-color-use-case-success-background` (`#d7f0d1`) · danger: `o3-color-use-case-error-text` (`#cc0000`) · warning: `o3-color-use-case-warning-background` (`#fff2cc`) · info: `o3-color-use-case-link-text` (`#0d7680`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"metric 2 VF", sans-serif` (top step `o3-font-size-10`: 5.25rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"metric 2 VF", sans-serif` (`o3-type-body-content-base-font-size`: 1.25rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (60)**: `o3-font-size-10` (5.25rem), `o3-font-size-9` (4.5rem), `o3-font-size-metric2-9` (4.5rem), `o3-font-size-metric2-8` (4rem), `o3-font-size-8` (3.5rem), `o3-type-display-lg-font-size` (3rem), `o3-font-size-7` (3rem), `o3-font-size-metric2-7` (3rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f4f4f5` cards; separate sibling sections using `#cccccc` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f4f4f5`) and `1px solid #cccccc` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff7faa` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff7faa` with `#0b0b0c` text (8.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `metric 2 VF` headings with `metric 2 VF` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f4f4f5` surface shifts and `#cccccc` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
