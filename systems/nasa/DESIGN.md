# NASA Web Design System

## Overview

**NASA Web Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff9d1e` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Helvetica Neue` with `System Sans` body copy (70px display down to 16px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f1f1f1` surface layering.

Maintained by **NASA**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://nasa.github.io/nasawds-site/). Inventory: **42** colors, **14** typography steps, and **24** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f1f1f1` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff9d1e` (2.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `h2-text` (`#9bdaf1`), `css-surface-8` (`#ffff00`), `css-surface-10` (`#061f4a`), `css-surface-11` (`#02bfe7`), `css-surface-12` (`#00a6d2`), `css-surface-13` (`#046b99`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `bg-canvas` (`#ffffff`), `canvas-text` (`#212121`), `link-text-1` (`#5b616b`), `css-color-9` (`#000000`), `css-color-14` (`#d6d7d9`), `css-surface-15` (`#323a45`).
- **Semantic States**: danger: `alert-bg` (`#ffebd1`) · info: `link-text` (`#0b3d91`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `display-alt`: 4.4rem, weight 300).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `sans-serif` (`html`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (14)**: `display-alt` (4.4rem, w300), `h2` (3.4rem, w700), `post-title` (3.4rem, w700), `h3` (2.4rem, w700), `display` (2.4rem, w700), `font-lead` (2.2rem, w300), `h1` (2em), `h4` (1.9rem, w700).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 24 component tokens with documented paddings: `2rem`, `0.1rem 0.7rem`, `1rem 2rem`, `0`, `7px 10px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f1f1f1` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f1f1f1`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff9d1e` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0px` (Buttons `5px`).
- **Strict Sharp Edges**: Maintain `0px` architectural corners across buttons, inputs, and cards.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `square` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff9d1e` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Helvetica Neue` headings with `System Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#f1f1f1` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-error-message` fields and secondary buttons.
