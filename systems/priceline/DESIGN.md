# Priceline Design System

## Overview

**Priceline Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ffa0c4` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Montserrat` (40px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `#f4f6f8` surface layering.

Maintained by **Priceline**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/priceline/design-system), repo https://github.com/priceline/design-system, package `pcln-design-system`, frameworks: react. Inventory: **80** colors, **7** typography steps, and **16** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f4f6f8` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ffa0c4` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#c0cad5` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `tint-blue` (`#99c3f9`), `blue` (`#0068ef`), `tone-blue` (`#0055c4`), `dark-blue` (`#049`), `shade-blue` (`#002f6d`), `tint-purple` (`#c28ce0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `black` (`#000`), `white` (`#fff`), `light-blue` (`#e8f2ff`), `light-green` (`#ecf7ec`), `light-red` (`#fbebeb`), `light-orange` (`#fef2e7`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `tint-green` (`#80d580`) · danger: `tint-red` (`#eb9999`) · warning: `tint-orange` (`#faaf69`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Montserrat, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (top step `font-size-6`: 40px, weight 700, line-height 1.2).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Montserrat, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (`font-size-2`: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.5).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `font-size-6` (40px, w700), `font-size-5` (32px, w700), `font-size-4` (24px, w700), `font-size-3` (20px, w600), `font-size-2` (16px, w400), `font-size-1` (14px, w400), `font-size-0` (12px, w500).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f4f6f8` cards; separate sibling sections using `#c0cad5` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#f4f6f8`) and `1px solid #c0cad5` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ffa0c4` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ffa0c4` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Montserrat` headings with `Montserrat` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f4f6f8` surface shifts and `#c0cad5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
