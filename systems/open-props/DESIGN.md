# Open Props

## Overview

**Open Props** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f8fafb` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.8:1 contrast) and `#0866ff` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (56px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#f8fafb` -> `#f8f0fc` surface layering.

Source: published token package, package `open-props`. Inventory: **91** colors, **49** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f8fafb` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f0fc` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.8:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0866ff` (4.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `0` (`#fff9db`), `8` (`#f08c00`), `brand-facebook` (`#0866ff`), `brand-twitter` (`#469cf0`), `brand-whatsapp` (`#25d366`), `brand-instagram-magenta` (`#d32dbf`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `stone-8` (`#666968`), `stone-0` (`#f8fafb`), `indigo-0` (`#edf2ff`), `gray-10` (`#16191d`), `gray-2` (`#e9ecef`), `gray4` (`#ced4da`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `brand-google-green` (`#0f9d58`) · danger: `brand-instagram-red` (`#e93365`) · warning: `brand-instagram-yellow` (`#f8d648`) · info: `link` (`#0065ff`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f0fc` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f8fafb` -> `#f8f0fc`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0866ff` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f8fafb` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0866ff` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f0fc` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
