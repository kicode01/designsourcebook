# Aviva Standards

## Overview

**Aviva Standards** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ffd900` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System Sans` (56px display down to 14px body), unified by `.25em` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Aviva**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://standards.aviva.com/). Inventory: **22** colors, **48** typography steps, and **44** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ffd900` (1.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 14.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#c6c6c6` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `header-bg` (`#cdebee`), `nav-text` (`#004fb6`), `nav-text-1` (`#1034bf`), `main-bg` (`#37afba`), `main-bg-2` (`#00251f`), `main-bg-3` (`#00596b`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `bg-canvas` (`#ffffff`), `canvas-text` (`#191919`), `nav-bg` (`#fffcf1`), `nav-border` (`#c6c6c6`), `h2-text` (`#444444`), `h3-text` (`#5c596d`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `sans-serif` (top step `a-heading-display-xl-desktop`: 3.5em, weight 600, line-height 1.14286em).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `sans-serif` (`ion-typescale-body1`: .875em, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 44 component tokens with documented paddings: `0`, `0 1em 1em`, `1em 2em`, `.75em 1.25em`, `.2858em 0`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#c6c6c6` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`inset 0 2px 2px 0 hsla(0,0%,80%,.5)`, `0 2px 2px 0 hsla(0,0%,80%,.5)`) paired with `#c6c6c6` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ffd900` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `.25em` (Buttons `10em` · Inputs `.25em` · Badges `.5em`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `.25em` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ffd900` with `#0b0b0c` text (14.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System Sans` headings with `System Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25em` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25em on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#c6c6c6` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25em` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `textarea` fields and secondary buttons.
