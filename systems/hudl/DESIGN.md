# Uniform

## Overview

**Uniform** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff6300` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `helvetica` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Hudl**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (http://uniform.hudl.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **8** typography steps, and **10** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff6300` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-button-minimal-secondary-label` (`#283c50`), `color-button-minimal-secondary-label-hover` (`#283c50`), `color-button-minimal-secondary-label-active` (`#283c50`), `color-button-minimal-primary-label` (`#00669e`), `color-button-standard-secondary-bg-active` (`#4b5d6c`), `color-button-standard-secondary-bg-hover` (`#5a6f81`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-button-minimal-primary-bg-hover` (`#cfdde2`), `color-brand-ink` (`#232a31`), `color-brand-evening` (`#38434f`), `color-bg-level3-accent` (`#353f4a`), `color-bg-level2-accent` (`#313b45`), `color-bg-level1-accent` (`#2c343e`).
- **Semantic States**: danger: `color-utility-critical-text` (`#ff5542`) · warning: `color-brand-orange` (`#ff6300`) · info: `color-interjection-information` (`#4d6680`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `helvetica, arial, sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 2em, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `helvetica, arial, sans-serif` (`font-size-default`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (8)**: `h1` (2em), `font-size-large` (1.125rem), `font-size-default` (1rem), `font-body` (16px), `body` (1rem), `font-size-small` (0.875rem), `font-size-micro` (0.75rem), `uni-item-title-small` (0.625rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 10 component tokens with documented paddings: `0px 24px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#fff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff6300` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `2px` · Inputs `2px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff6300` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `helvetica` headings with `helvetica` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
