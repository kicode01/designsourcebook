# Wonder Blocks

## Overview

**Wonder Blocks** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFFFFF` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#1b50b3` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Plus Jakarta Sans` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Khan Academy**. Source: published token package, package `@khanacademy/wonder-blocks-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **7** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFFFFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1b50b3` (7.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 6.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#dbdcdd` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `wb-semantic-color-core-border-instructive-subtle` (`#b5cefb`), `wb-semantic-color-core-background-base-strong` (`#0b2149`), `wb-semantic-color-graphics-external-brands-google-gr` (`#34A853`), `wb-semantic-color-graphics-role-parent-background` (`#BCEBBB`), `wb-semantic-color-learning-background-gems-strong` (`#84275E`), `wb-semantic-color-learning-background-streaks-subtle` (`#FEE9E5`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#dbdcdd` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#FFFFFF` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid #dbdcdd` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1b50b3` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.

- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFFFFF` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#1b50b3` with `#f7f7f8` text (6.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Plus Jakarta Sans` headings with `Plus Jakarta Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#dbdcdd` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
