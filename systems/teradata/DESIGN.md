# Covalent

## Overview

**Covalent** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#3053f4` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (96px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f8f9fb` surface layering.

Maintained by **Teradata**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/Teradata/covalent), repo https://github.com/Teradata/covalent. Inventory: **91** colors, **91** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f9fb` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#3053f4` (5.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 5.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `neutral-20` (`#3e4557ff`), `neutral-21` (`#41485bff`), `secondary-container` (`#DEEAFA`), `ctions-theme-cv-theme-palettes-secondary80-dark` (`#C3C5DD`), `collections-theme-cv-theme-palettes-neutral-22-light` (`#444b5f`), `collections-theme-cv-theme-palettes-neutral-35-light` (`#66708c`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `0` (`#ffffff`), `neutral-100` (`#ffffffff`), `neutral-97` (`#f6f7fbff`), `neutral-9` (`#1b1f29ff`), `neutral-variant-94` (`#ededf1ff`), `neutral-variant-35` (`#515256ff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `positive-60` (`#358533ff`) · danger: `cv-dark-on-error` (`#331915`) · warning: `orange-20` (`#8d3001ff`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter` (top step `headline1-font-size`: 96px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter` (`body1-font-size`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Roboto Mono", monospace` (`code-font-family`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (91)**: `headline1-font-size` (96px), `cv-typography-headline1-font-size` (96px), `headline2-font-size` (60px), `cv-typography-headline2-font-size` (60px), `headline3-font-size` (48px), `headline1-article-font-size` (48px), `collections-typography-cv-typography-headline1-font-` (48px), `lections-typography-cv-typography-headline1-article-` (48px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f9fb` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f8f9fb`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#3053f4` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `32x32` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#3053f4` with `#f7f7f8` text (5.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f9fb` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
