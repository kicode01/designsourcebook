# SEED Design System

## Overview

**SEED Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#f60` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (72px display down to 16px body), unified by `20px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Daangn**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/daangn/seed-design), repo https://github.com/daangn/seed-design. Inventory: **91** colors, **33** typography steps, and **56** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f8f9` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#f60` (2.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `seed-color-manner-temp-l10-text` (`#cb0123`), `seed-color-manner-temp-l2-text` (`#ab863f`), `seed-color-manner-temp-l3-text` (`#e08a00`), `seed-color-manner-temp-l4-text` (`#f57e00`), `seed-color-manner-temp-l5-text` (`#ff7300`), `seed-color-manner-temp-l6-text` (`#ff6600`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `seed-color-manner-temp-l1-text` (`#757b85`), `seed-color-fg-disabled` (`#d1d3d8`), `seed-color-fg-neutral` (`#1a1c20`), `seed-color-fg-neutral-inverted` (`#fff`), `seed-color-fg-neutral-muted` (`#555d6d`), `seed-color-fg-neutral-subtle` (`#868b94`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `seed-color-palette-green-200` (`#d9f6e9`) · danger: `seed-color-palette-red-600` (`#fc6a66`) · warning: `seed-color-fg-warning` (`#9b7821`) · info: `seed-color-fg-informative` (`#217cf9`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `system-ui` (top step `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-1300`: 4.5rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `system-ui` (`seed-font-size-t5-static`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (33)**: `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-1300` (4.5rem), `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-1200` (3.75rem), `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-1100` (3.375rem), `seed-font-size-t14-static` (48px), `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-1000` (3rem), `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-900` (2.625rem), `seed-font-size-t13-static` (40px), `seed-scale-dimension-font-size-800` (2.125rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 56 component tokens with documented paddings: `0`, `10px 14px`, `6px 10px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f8f9` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`inset 0 -1px #00000010`, `inset 0 0 0 1px #000f3323`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#f60` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `20px` (Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `20px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#f60` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `20px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (20px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f8f9` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `20px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `seed-field-label` fields and secondary buttons.
