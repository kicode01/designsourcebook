# NutUI

## Overview

**NutUI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#f82c45` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (30px display down to 14px body), unified by `21px` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `rgba(245, 245, 245, 1)` surface layering.

Maintained by **JD.com**. Source: published token package, package `@nutui/nutui`. Inventory: **91** colors, **153** typography steps, and **40** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgba(245, 245, 245, 1)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#808080` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#f82c45` (3.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `rgba(204, 204, 204, 1)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `tdd-color-brand-normal` (`#f82c45`), `tdd-color-brand-gradient-normal-2` (`#f82c45`), `dark-background6` (`#380e08`), `numberkeyboard-key-active-finsh-background-color` (`#0570db`), `primary-color-end` (`#fa6419`), `nut-tdd-color-brand-normal` (`#f82c45`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `text-color` (`#808080`), `dark-background2` (`#1b1b1b`), `dark-background4` (`#323233`), `button-default-border-color` (`rgba(204, 204, 204, 1)`), `divider-text-color` (`#909ca4`), `input-border-bottom` (`#eaf0fb`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `button-success-border-color` (`rgba(38, 191, 38, 1)`) · danger: `button-danger-background-color` (`rgba(250, 44, 25, 1)`) · warning: `tdd-color-warning` (`#de6a1c`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `system-ui` (top step `tdd-font-size-1`: 30px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `system-ui` (`steps-base-title-font-size`: 14px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `system-ui` (`elevator-list-item-code-font-size`: 14px).
- **Scale Steps (153)**: `tdd-font-size-1` (30px), `font-size` (30px), `nut-tdd-font-size-1` (30px), `numberkeyboard-key-font-size` (28px), `nut-numberkeyboard-key-font-size` (28px), `tdd-font-size-2` (24px), `nut-tdd-font-size-2` (24px), `tdd-font-size-3` (20px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 40 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 25px`, `24px 20px`, `24px 30px`, `5px 18px`, `0`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgba(245, 245, 245, 1)` cards; separate sibling sections using `rgba(204, 204, 204, 1)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `rgba(245, 245, 245, 1)`) and `1px solid rgba(204, 204, 204, 1)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#f82c45` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `21px` (Buttons `20%` · Navigation `2px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `21px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#808080` secondary labels.
- Use `#f82c45` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `21px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (21px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgba(245, 245, 245, 1)` surface shifts and `rgba(204, 204, 204, 1)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `21px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
