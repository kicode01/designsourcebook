# Box UI Elements

## Overview

**Box UI Elements** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#004eac` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (30px display down to 18px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `#fbfbfb` surface layering.

Maintained by **Box**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/box/box-ui-elements), repo https://github.com/box/box-ui-elements. Inventory: **91** colors, **30** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fbfbfb` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#004eac` (7.8:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 7.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `count-badge-background` (`#e9163c`), `primary-background-active` (`#004eac`), `primary-background-gradient` (`#0055bc`), `primary-background-hover` (`#006ae9`), `primary-button-background-active` (`#004eac`), `primary-button-background-hover` (`#006ae9`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `hover-blue-background` (`#f6fafd`), `hover-blue-border` (`#d9e3e9`), `selected-blue-background` (`#ecf4fc`), `white` (`#fff`), `bdl-box-blue-10` (`#e5effa`), `bdl-box-blue-02` (`#fafcfe`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `bdl-green-light-50` (`#92e0c0`) · danger: `bdl-watermelon-red-50` (`#f69bab`) · warning: `bdl-yellorange-50` (`#fad98d`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `system-ui` (top step `h1`: 30px, weight 600, line-height 36px).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `system-ui` (`bdl-preview-title-body-two-buttons-modal-template-ti`: 18px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (30)**: `h1` (30px), `h2` (24px), `h3` (20px), `bdl-preview-title-body-two-buttons-modal-template-ti` (18px), `message-text-content-title` (18px), `html` (100%), `h4` (16px), `bdl-guide-tooltip-title` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fbfbfb` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#fbfbfb`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#004eac` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#004eac` with `#f7f7f8` text (7.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fbfbfb` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
