# ONS Design System

## Overview

**ONS Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#e2b500` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `OpenSans` (24px display down to 14px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Office for National Statistics**. Source: published token package, package `@ons/design-system`. Inventory: **90** colors, **7** typography steps, and **30** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f2f2f2` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#e2b500` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#cccccc` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `background-color` (`#003B61`), `ons-color-night-blue` (`#003c57`), `ons-color-ocean-blue` (`#206095`), `ons-color-sky-blue` (`#27a0cc`), `ons-color-aqua-teal` (`#00a3a6`), `ons-color-navy-blue` (`#194766`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `data-labels-border-color` (`#cccccc`), `dial-background-color` (`#000000`), `dial-border-color` (`#cccccc`), `pivot-border-color` (`#cccccc`), `pivot-background-color` (`#000000`), `style-background-color` (`#ffffff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `ons-color-spring-green` (`#a8bd3a`) · danger: `ons-color-ruby-red` (`#d0021b`) · warning: `ons-color-jaffa-orange` (`#fa6401`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `OpenSans, "Helvetica Neue", arial, sans-serif` (top step `ons-details-title`: 24px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `OpenSans, "Helvetica Neue", arial, sans-serif` (`default-font-size`: 0.875rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `RobotoMono, monospace` (`font-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `ons-details-title` (24px), `font-sans` (16px), `font-serif` (16px), `font-mono` (16px), `font-family` (16px), `default-font-size` (0.875rem), `font-size` (0.7em).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 30 component tokens with documented paddings: `0`, `.875rem 1rem`, `.5rem 0`, `.75rem 1rem`, `.8rem 0`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f2f2f2` cards; separate sibling sections using `#cccccc` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0.1875rem 0 #073d20`, `0 -2px #fbc900,0 4px #222`) paired with `#cccccc` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#e2b500` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0px` (Buttons `0`).
- **Strict Sharp Edges**: Maintain `0px` architectural corners across buttons, inputs, and cards.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#e2b500` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `OpenSans` headings with `OpenSans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#f2f2f2` surface shifts and `#cccccc` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-type` fields and secondary buttons.
