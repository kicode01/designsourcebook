# AXA Slash Design System

## Overview

**AXA Slash Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0275d8` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Georgia` with `Source Sans Pro` body copy (88px display down to 16px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **AXA**. Source: published token package, package `@axa-fr/react-toolkit-all`. Inventory: **21** colors, **25** typography steps, and **56** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f9fa` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0275d8` (4.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `blue` (`#0275d8`), `indigo` (`#6610f2`), `purple` (`#613d7c`), `pink` (`#ff5b77`), `teal` (`#3a87ad`), `cyan` (`#17a2b8`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `white` (`#fff`), `gray` (`#6c757d`), `gray-dark` (`#343a40`), `secondary` (`#6c757d`), `light` (`#f8f9fa`), `dark` (`#343a40`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green` (`#468847`) · danger: `red` (`#d90000`) · warning: `orange` (`#e18a00`) · info: `info` (`#17a2b8`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Georgia, Times, "Times New Roman", serif` (top step `display-2`: 5.5rem, weight 400, line-height 1.2).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Source Sans Pro", Arial, sans-serif` (`body`: 1rem, weight 400, line-height 1.5).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", "Courier New", monospace` (`font-family-monospace`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (25)**: `display-2` (5.5rem, w400), `display-4` (3.5rem, w400), `af-tabs-title` (2.25rem, w700), `af-subtitle` (2em), `af-title-content` (1.75em, w600), `af-help-demo-title` (1.5rem, w400), `af-accordion-item-title` (1.4em, w200), `af-rccard-header-title` (1.375rem, w600).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 56 component tokens with documented paddings: `.375rem .75rem`, `.8rem 1.2rem .8rem 1.2rem`, `.2rem .7rem`, `.5rem .75rem`, `.75rem 1rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f9fa` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 .25rem .75rem rgba(0,0,0,.1)`, `inset 0 -3px #00008f`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0275d8` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `.25rem` (Buttons `.25rem` · Cards `.25rem` · Badges `16px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0275d8` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Georgia` headings with `Source Sans Pro` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f9fa` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
