# Canvas Design System

## Overview

**Canvas Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#fcc6b1` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `HubSpot Serif` with `HubSpot Sans` body copy (112px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f8f5ee` surface layering.

Maintained by **Hubspot**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://canvas.hubspot.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **24** typography steps, and **9** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f5ee` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#fcc6b1` (1.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 13.0:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#f8f5ee` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `light-theme-accent-fill-03` (`#fcc5be`), `light-theme-accent-fill-07` (`#b2e9eb`), `light-theme-accent-decoration-03` (`#ffa499`), `light-theme-accent-decoration-05` (`#ccc0a3`), `light-theme-accent-decoration-07` (`#97dadc`), `light-theme-badge-brand-fill-01` (`#fcc6b1`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `light-theme-accent-fill-01` (`#cfcccb`), `light-theme-accent-fill-05` (`#ece6d9`), `light-theme-accent-decoration-01` (`#9b9897`), `light-theme-background-02` (`#f8f5ee`), `light-theme-background-accent-01` (`#b9cdbe`), `light-theme-button-primary-text-color` (`#ffffff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `light-theme-success-01` (`#00823a`) · danger: `light-theme-error-background-01` (`#fcc5be`) · warning: `light-theme-warning-01` (`#eeb117`) · info: `light-theme-text-link-underline-01` (`#ff4800`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"HubSpot Serif", serif` (top step `cl-font-size-display-01`: 7rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"HubSpot Sans", sans-serif` (`cl-font-size-medium`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (24)**: `cl-font-size-display-01` (7rem), `cl-font-size-display-02` (4.5rem), `cl-font-size-display-01-small` (3.5rem), `cl-font-size-display-02-small` (3.5rem), `cl-font-size-h1` (3rem), `cl-font-size-h1-small` (2.5rem), `cl-font-size-h2` (2.5rem), `cl-font-size-h2-small` (2rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 9 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f5ee` cards; separate sibling sections using `#f8f5ee` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f8f5ee`) and `1px solid #f8f5ee` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#fcc6b1` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#fcc6b1` with `#0b0b0c` text (13.0:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `HubSpot Serif` headings with `HubSpot Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f5ee` surface shifts and `#f8f5ee` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
