# Mayflower Design System

## Overview

**Mayflower Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#e8eef4` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (16.8:1 contrast) and `mds-color-brand-gray-700` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Noto Sans` (168px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Massachusetts**. Source: published token package, package `@massds/mds-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **23** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#e8eef4` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#e8eef4` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (16.8:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `mds-color-brand-gray-700` (distinct vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for verified contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-200` (`#d0dde9`), `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-300` (`#b9ccdd`), `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-400` (`#a1bbd2`), `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-500` (`#8aaac7`), `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-600` (`#7299bc`), `mds-color-brand-bay-blue-700` (`#5b88b1`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#e8eef4` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `mds-color-brand-gray-700` with `#f7f7f8` text (verified) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Noto Sans` headings with `Noto Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#e8eef4` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
