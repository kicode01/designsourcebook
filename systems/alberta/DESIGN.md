# Alberta Design System

## Overview

**Alberta Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0081a2` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `acumin-variable` (48px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f2f0f0` surface layering.

Maintained by **Government of Alberta**. Source: published token package, package `@abgov/design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **20** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see package license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f2f0f0` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0081a2` (4.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#cdcdcd` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `secondary` (`#e0f1ff`), `brand` (`#0081a2`), `button-secondary-focus-color-text` (`#85b5d8`), `goa-color-extended-prairie-text` (`#47372a`), `goa-color-extended-dawn-border` (`#e9b8d5`), `goa-color-extended-sky-text-dark` (`#093937`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `0` (`#f8f8f8`), `500` (`#808080`), `callout-l-info-content-bg-color` (`#1e2830`), `footer-color-links-hover` (`#aaaaaa`), `goa-color-surface-app-header` (`#ffffff`), `goa-color-surface-250` (`#f2f0f0`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `goa-color-success-text-dark` (`#355147`) · danger: `radio-color-bg-error-hover` (`#f4c8c5`) · warning: `warning` (`#f9ce2d`) · info: `goa-color-info-dark` (`#004a8f`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `acumin-variable, helvetica-neue, arial, sans-serif` (top step `goa-font-size-10`: 3rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `acumin-variable, helvetica-neue, arial, sans-serif` (`badge-font-size-large`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `roboto-mono, monospace` (`goa-font-family-number`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (20)**: `goa-font-size-10` (3rem), `goa-font-size-9` (2.5rem), `goa-font-size-8` (2rem), `goa-font-size-7` (1.75rem), `goa-font-size-6` (1.5rem), `goa-font-size-5` (1.25rem), `goa-font-size-05` (1.125rem), `goa-font-size-4` (1.125rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f2f0f0` cards; separate sibling sections using `#cdcdcd` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f2f0f0`) and `1px solid #cdcdcd` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0081a2` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0081a2` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `acumin-variable` headings with `acumin-variable` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f2f0f0` surface shifts and `#cdcdcd` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
