# Alfa Design System

## Overview

**Alfa Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ef3124` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Alfa-Bank**. Source: published token package, package `@alfalab/core-components-vars`. Inventory: **91** colors, **4** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ef3124` (4.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#d5d6dc` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-dark-text-attention-inverted-press` (`#b45a06`), `color-light-text-attention-press` (`#b45a06`), `color-light-bg-accent` (`#ef3124`), `color-dark-bg-accent-shade-15` (`rgb(203, 42, 31)`), `color-light-bg-accent-shade-7` (`rgb(222, 46, 33)`), `color-light-graphic-accent-shade-7` (`rgb(222, 46, 33)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-static-corpbg-neutral` (`#bdc0c2`), `color-dark-accent-secondary` (`#f2f3f5`), `color-dark-base-bg-alt-secondary-inverted` (`#fff`), `color-dark-modal-bg-primary` (`#1c1c1e`), `color-dark-neutral-0-press` (`#353539`), `color-dark-neutral-1300-inverted` (`#212124`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-dark-text-positive-inverted-press` (`#036625`) · danger: `color-dark-text-negative-hover` (`#ff755e`) · warning: `color-dark-decorative-text-orange` (`#fa9313`) · info: `color-dark-status-info-inverted-press` (`#2c64d4`).

- **Body & UI Controls**: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` (`font-family-system`: 16px, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#d5d6dc` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#fff`) and `1px solid #d5d6dc` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ef3124` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.

- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ef3124` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `#d5d6dc` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
