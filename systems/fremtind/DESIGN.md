# Jokul

## Overview

**Jokul** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `brand-fjellgra` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (56px display down to 20px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f9f9f9` surface layering.

Maintained by **Fremtind Forsikring**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/fremtind/jokul/tree/pre-1.0/packages/core), package `@fremtind/jkl-core`. Inventory: **91** colors, **87** typography steps, and **9** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f9f9f9` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `brand-fjellgra` (distinct vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for verified contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `suksess` (`#acd3b5`), `feil-dark` (`#de9e9e`), `color-suksess-dark` (`#94b79b`), `color-advarsel` (`#efdd9e`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `brand-snohvit` (`#F9F9F9`), `brand-hvit` (`#FFFFFF`), `brand-fjellgra` (`#444141`), `brand-dis` (`#ECE9E5`), `neutral-light` (`#E0DBD4`), `color-brand-snohvit` (`#f9f9f9`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `jkl-success` (`#acd3b5`) · danger: `functional-error-dark` (`#DE9E9E`) · warning: `functional-warning` (`#EFDD9E`) · info: `functional-info-dark` (`#A9A9CA`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `system-ui` (top step `typography-font-size-56`: 3.5rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `system-ui` (`typography-body-base-font-size`: 1.25rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (87)**: `typography-font-size-56` (3.5rem), `typography-title-base-font-size` (3.5rem), `jkl-typography-font-size-56` (3.5rem), `jkl-typography-title-base-font-size` (3.5rem), `jkl-title-font-size` (3.5rem), `typography-font-size-44` (2.75rem), `typography-title-small-base-font-size` (2.75rem), `jkl-typography-font-size-44` (2.75rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 9 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f9f9f9` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f9f9f9`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `brand-fjellgra` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `brand-fjellgra` with `#f7f7f8` text (verified) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f9f9f9` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
