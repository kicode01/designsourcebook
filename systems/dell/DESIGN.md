# Dell Design System

## Overview

**Dell Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff99a1` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Roboto` (72px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#ffebf5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Dell**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://www.delldesignsystem.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **20** typography steps, and **11** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffebf5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff99a1` (2.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `dds-color-berry-20` (`#ffc0df`), `dds-color-berry-30` (`#f69ac6`), `dds-color-berry-40` (`#e574aa`), `dds-color-berry-50` (`#cb548d`), `dds-color-berry-60` (`#ba467d`), `dds-color-berry-70` (`#a8396f`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `dds-color-neutral-black` (`#000000`), `dds-color-neutral-white` (`#ffffff`), `dds-color-berry-10` (`#ffebf5`), `dds-color-gray-10` (`#f5f6f7`), `dds-color-gray-20` (`#f0f0f0`), `dds-color-gray-30` (`#e1e1e1`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `dds-color-green-10` (`#e9f5ce`) · danger: `dds-color-red-20` (`#ffc3c9`) · warning: `dds-color-orange-10` (`#ffeed2`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Roboto` (top step `dds-font-size-72`: 4.5rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Roboto` (`dds-typography-font-family-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Roboto Mono"` (`dds-font-family-roboto-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (20)**: `dds-font-size-72` (4.5rem), `dds-font-size-64` (4rem), `dds-font-size-56` (3.5rem), `dds-font-size-48` (3rem), `dds-font-size-40` (2.5rem), `dds-font-size-32` (2rem), `dds-font-size-24` (1.5rem), `dds-font-size-20` (1.25rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 11 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `6px 10px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffebf5` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffebf5`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff99a1` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Cards `0.25rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `0.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff99a1` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Roboto` headings with `Roboto` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (0.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffebf5` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
