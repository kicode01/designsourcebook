# KERN UX

## Overview

**KERN UX** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f3f4f7` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (17.9:1 contrast) and `brand-colorpalette-1-300` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Fira Sans` (80px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Germany Federal Government**. Source: published token package (https://kern-ux.de), package `@kern-ux/native`. Inventory: **91** colors, **26** typography steps, and **30** documented components (EUPL-1.2 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f3f4f7` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f3f4f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (17.9:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `brand-colorpalette-1-300` (distinct vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for verified contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `neutral-300` (`#a9aec6`), `neutral-400` (`#8e93b1`), `neutral-500` (`#6e7597`), `neutral-600` (`#5b6185`), `neutral-700` (`#454b6b`), `neutral-800` (`#30344f`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `neutral-025` (`#f3f4f7`), `neutral-100` (`#dfe1ea`), `neutral-200` (`#c0c3d5`), `neutral-900` (`#1d2034`), `brand-colorpalette-1-025` (`#f2f2fb`), `brand-colorpalette-1-100` (`#dfe0f5`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green-250` (`#8ac593`) · danger: `red-150` (`#fdc5bb`) · warning: `yellow-200` (`#e9c00d`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 30 component tokens with documented paddings: `0rem`, `0`, `0.5rem 1.5rem 1rem 1.5rem`, `0.375rem 0rem 0.125rem 0rem`, `1rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f3f4f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`inset 0 -1px 0 0 #6E7597`, `inset 0 -1px 0 0 #6E7597`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `brand-colorpalette-1-300` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Inputs `0.125rem` · Cards `0.25rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `0.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f3f4f7` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `brand-colorpalette-1-300` with `#f7f7f8` text (verified) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Fira Sans` headings with `Fira Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (0.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f3f4f7` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `kern-form-input` fields and secondary buttons.
