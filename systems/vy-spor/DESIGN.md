# Spor

## Overview

**Spor** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFFFFF` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#76A300` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Vy Sans` (54px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#FFFFFF` -> `#F5F5F5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Vy Group**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/nsbno/spor/tree/main/packages/spor-card-react), package `@vygruppen/spor-design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **33** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFFFFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F5F5F5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#76A300` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `tog` (`#123DA0`), `lokaltog` (`#65B152`), `lokaltog-light` (`#E5F6D1`), `regiontog` (`#C34C4C`), `region-ekspress` (`#10A8B2`), `region-ekspress-light` (`#D8F1F4`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `50` (`#F5F5F5`), `100` (`#EBEBEC`), `200` (`#D7D8D9`), `300` (`#AFB2B3`), `400` (`#888B8E`), `500` (`#606568`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-palette-green-100` (`#CCEAE4`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Vy Sans"` (top step `font-style-xxl-font-size-desktop`: 3.375rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Vy Sans"` (`font-family-body`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Roboto Mono"` (`font-family-monospace`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (33)**: `font-style-xxl-font-size-desktop` (3.375rem), `font-style-xl-display-font-size-desktop` (2.5rem), `font-style-xl-sans-font-size-desktop` (2.5rem), `font-style-xxl-font-size-mobile` (2.25rem), `font-style-xl-display-font-size-mobile` (1.875rem), `font-style-xl-sans-font-size-mobile` (1.875rem), `font-style-lg-font-size-desktop` (1.875rem), `font-style-lg-font-size-mobile` (1.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F5F5F5` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#FFFFFF` -> `#F5F5F5`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#76A300` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFFFFF` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#76A300` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Vy Sans` headings with `Vy Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F5F5F5` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
