# Wanda Design System

## Overview

**Wanda Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `hsl(0 0% 100%)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#e00000` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `token(--wds-font-family-sans)` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `hsl(0 0% 100%)` -> `hsl(220 20% 98%)` surface layering.

Maintained by **Wonderflow**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/wonderflow-bv/wanda), repo https://github.com/wonderflow-bv/wanda. Inventory: **91** colors, **3** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `hsl(0 0% 100%)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `hsl(220 20% 98%)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#e00000` (5.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `10` (`#a7e2ff`), `30` (`#458bd0`), `50` (`#12499f`), `70` (`#00246f`), `90` (`#00123e`), `brand` (`#e00000`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `0` (`#e7ffff`), `primary-0` (`hsl(220 20% 98%)`), `primary-5` (`hsl(220 19% 91%)`), `primary-10` (`hsl(220 16% 85%)`), `primary-20` (`hsl(219 14% 75%)`), `primary-30` (`hsl(220 12% 66%)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green-0` (`hsl(149 100% 91%)`) · danger: `red-10` (`hsl(351 100% 81%)`) · warning: `yellow-0` (`hsl(60 100% 89%)`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"token(--wds-font-family-sans)"` (top step `html`: 100%, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"token(--wds-font-family-sans)"` (`body`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (3)**: `html` (100%), `body` (16px), `font-family` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `hsl(220 20% 98%)` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`hsl(0 0% 100%)` -> `hsl(220 20% 98%)`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#e00000` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `hsl(0 0% 100%)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#e00000` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `token(--wds-font-family-sans)` headings with `token(--wds-font-family-sans)` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `hsl(220 20% 98%)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
