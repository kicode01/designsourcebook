# One UI

## Overview

**One UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#007bff` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Samsung SS Head` with `System UI` body copy (96px display down to 16px body), unified by `0.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Samsung**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://developer.samsung.com/one-ui). Inventory: **24** colors, **23** typography steps, and **78** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f9fa` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#007bff` (4.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `blue` (`#00B3E3`), `indigo` (`#6610f2`), `purple` (`#6f42c1`), `pink` (`#e83e8c`), `teal` (`#20c997`), `cyan` (`#17a2b8`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `white` (`#fff`), `gray` (`#6c757d`), `gray-dark` (`#343a40`), `secondary` (`#6c757d`), `light` (`#f8f9fa`), `dark` (`#343a40`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green` (`#28a745`) · danger: `red` (`#dc3545`) · warning: `orange` (`#fd7e14`) · info: `info` (`#17a2b8`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 78 component tokens with documented paddings: `.5rem`, `0`, `1.4375rem`, `100px 120px`, `0 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8f9fa` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 10px 40px rgba(5,40,50,.1)`, `0 0.25rem 0.75rem rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#007bff` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0.25rem` (Buttons `0` · Inputs `1rem` · Cards `.875rem` · Badges `0` · Navigation `0.25rem`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#007bff` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Samsung SS Head` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `0.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.875rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8f9fa` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `0.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `accordion-list-v3-search-box` fields and secondary buttons.
