# SubZero

## Overview

**SubZero** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFFFFF` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#97144D` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `400 1rem/1.5 Lato` (76px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Axis Bank**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://www.subzero.axis.bank.in/). Inventory: **91** colors, **98** typography steps, and **9** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFFFFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#FFFFFF` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#97144D` (8.3:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 7.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `palette-primary-main` (`#97144D`), `palette-primary-light` (`rgb(171, 67, 112)`), `palette-primary-dark` (`rgb(105, 14, 53)`), `palette-secondary-main` (`#ED1164`), `palette-secondary-light` (`rgb(240, 64, 131)`), `palette-secondary-dark` (`rgb(165, 11, 70)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `palette-common-background` (`#FFFFFF`), `palette-common-on-background` (`#282828`), `palette-primary-contrast-text` (`#FFFFFF`), `palette-secondary-contrast-text` (`#FFFFFF`), `palette-tertiary-contrast-text` (`#FFFFFF`), `palette-error-contrast-text` (`#FFFFFF`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 9 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#FFFFFF` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#FFFFFF` -> `#FFFFFF`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#97144D` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFFFFF` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#97144D` with `#f7f7f8` text (7.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `400 1rem/1.5 Lato` headings with `400 1rem/1.5 Lato` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#FFFFFF` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
