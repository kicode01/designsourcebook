# Tailwind CSS

## Overview

**Tailwind CSS** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fbfbf9` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.0:1 contrast) and `#fe9a00` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fbfbf9` -> `#f0f9ff` surface layering.

Maintained by **Tailwind Labs**. Source: published token package (https://tailwindcss.com), package `tailwindcss`. Inventory: **91** colors, **3** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fbfbf9` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f0f9ff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#fe9a00` (2.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-lime-100` (`#ecfcca`), `color-lime-400` (`#9ae600`), `color-lime-700` (`#497d00`), `color-lime-950` (`#192e03`), `color-emerald-300` (`#5ee9b5`), `color-emerald-600` (`#009966`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-red-50` (`#fef2f2`), `color-yellow-50` (`#fefce8`), `color-emerald-50` (`#ecfdf5`), `color-sky-50` (`#f0f9ff`), `color-violet-50` (`#f5f3ff`), `color-purple-100` (`#f3e8ff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-green-200` (`#b9f8cf`) · danger: `color-red-300` (`#ffa2a2`) · warning: `color-orange-100` (`#ffedd4`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f0f9ff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fbfbf9` -> `#f0f9ff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#fe9a00` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fbfbf9` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#fe9a00` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f0f9ff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
