# Talend Design System

## Overview

**Talend Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.4:1 contrast) and `hsla(204, 96%, 18%, 1)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Source Sans Pro` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `0.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Talend**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/Talend/ui/tree/master/packages/design-tokens#readme), package `@talend/design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **2** typography steps, and **18** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.4:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `hsla(204, 96%, 18%, 1)` (11.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 11.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `hsla(0, 0%, 65%, 1)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `coral-color-accent-text-weak-hover` (`hsla(204, 59%, 88%, 1)`), `coral-color-accent-text-hover` (`hsla(204, 96%, 18%, 1)`), `coral-color-accent-background-hover` (`hsla(205, 60%, 75%, 1)`), `coral-color-accent-background-weak-active` (`hsla(205, 60%, 75%, 1)`), `coral-color-accent-background-strong-active` (`hsla(205, 95%, 15%, 1)`), `coral-color-success-background` (`hsla(110, 49%, 90%, 1)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `coral-color-neutral-text` (`hsla(0, 0%, 13%, 1)`), `coral-color-neutral-background-medium` (`hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)`), `coral-color-neutral-border` (`hsla(0, 0%, 55%, 1)`), `coral-color-assistive-background` (`hsla(210, 62%, 5%, 1)`), `coral-color-neutral-border-disabled` (`hsla(0, 0%, 65%, 1)`), `coral-color-neutral-icon` (`hsla(0, 0%, 13%, 1)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `coral-color-success-text` (`hsla(111, 49%, 34%, 1)`) · danger: `coral-color-danger-text-weak-hover` (`hsla(358, 100%, 94%, 1)`) · warning: `coral-color-warning-text-weak-active` (`hsla(22, 85%, 82%, 1)`) · info: `coral-color-info-text` (`hsla(204, 95%, 31%, 1)`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Source Sans Pro"` (top step `font-family`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Source Sans Pro"` (`font-family`: 16px, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 18 component tokens with documented paddings: `2.25rem`, `0 0 0 0.5rem`, `0`, `0.5rem`, `0 0.5rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` cards; separate sibling sections using `hsla(0, 0%, 65%, 1)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0.125rem 0.375rem 0 hsla(0, 0%, 0%, 0.3)`, `0 0.125rem 0.375rem 0 hsla(0, 0%, 0%, 0.3)`) paired with `hsla(0, 0%, 65%, 1)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `hsla(204, 96%, 18%, 1)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0.25rem` (Cards `0.5rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `0.5rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `hsla(204, 96%, 18%, 1)` with `#f7f7f8` text (11.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Source Sans Pro` headings with `Source Sans Pro` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `0.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (0.5rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` surface shifts and `hsla(0, 0%, 65%, 1)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `0.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `Input-module` fields and secondary buttons.
