# Codex

## Overview

**Codex** is a dark-first design system anchored on `#202122` canvas surfaces with `#f7f7f8` primary ink (15.1:1 contrast) and `#36c` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System Sans` (28px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Wikimedia**. Source: published token package (https://doc.wikimedia.org/codex/), package `@wikimedia/codex-design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **33** typography steps, and **8** documented components (GPL-2.0+ license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#202122` — base application backdrop (dark theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#202122` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#f7f7f8` — body copy and primary headings (15.1:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#f7f7f8` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#36c` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 5.0:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `box-shadow-color-progressive-focus` (`#6485d1`), `background-color-progressive-subtle-hover` (`#233566`), `background-color-content-added` (`#233566`), `background-color-option-blue` (`#233566`), `border-color-progressive` (`#6485d1`), `border-color-content-removed` (`#987027`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-neutral` (`#c8ccd1`), `background-color-neutral-subtle` (`#202122`), `background-color-interactive-subtle` (`#202122`), `background-color-disabled-subtle` (`#27292d`), `border-color-base` (`#72777d`), `border-color-interactive-hover` (`#a2a9b1`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `border-color-option-green` (`#099979`) · danger: `color-error-hover` (`#fea898`) · warning: `background-color-option-orange` (`#572c19`) · info: `link-active` (`#b6d4fb`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `sans-serif` (top step `font-size-xxx-large`: 1.75rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `sans-serif` (`font-family-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", "Fira Code", "Courier New", monospace` (`font-family-monospace`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (33)**: `font-size-xxx-large` (1.75rem), `cdx-font-size-xxx-large` (1.75rem), `font-size-xx-large` (1.5rem), `cdx-font-size-xx-large` (1.5rem), `font-size-x-large` (1.25rem), `cdx-font-size-xlarge` (1.25rem), `font-size-large` (1.125rem), `cdx-font-size-large` (1.125rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#202122` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#202122` -> `#202122`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#36c` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#202122` (dark) with `#f7f7f8` primary text and `#f7f7f8` secondary labels.
- Use `#36c` with `#f7f7f8` text (5.0:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System Sans` headings with `System Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#202122` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of dark polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
