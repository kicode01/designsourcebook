# WordPress Design System

## Overview

**WordPress Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#3858e9` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **WordPress**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/tree/HEAD/packages/theme/README.md), package `@wordpress/theme`. Inventory: **90** colors, **9** typography steps, and **8** documented components (GPL-2.0-or-later license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#3858e9` (5.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 5.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `wpds-color-background-interactive-brand-strong` (`#3858e9`), `wpds-color-background-interactive-error-weak-active` (`#fae5e1`), `wpds-color-background-thumb-brand` (`#3858e9`), `wpds-color-foreground-interactive-brand-active` (`#0b0070`), `wpds-color-stroke-focus` (`#3858e9`), `wpds-color-stroke-interactive-brand-active` (`#2337c8`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `wpds-color-background-interactive-brand-strong-disab` (`#e6e6e6`), `wpds-color-background-interactive-error-active` (`#fff6f5`), `wpds-color-background-interactive-neutral-active` (`#fff`), `wpds-color-background-interactive-neutral-strong` (`#2d2d2d`), `wpds-color-background-interactive-neutral-strong-dis` (`#e6e6e6`), `wpds-color-background-surface-brand` (`#ecf0fa`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `wpds-color-background-surface-success` (`#c2f8ca`) · danger: `wpds-color-background-interactive-error-strong-activ` (`#b90000`) · warning: `wpds-color-background-surface-caution-weak` (`#fff9ca`) · info: `wpds-color-foreground-content-info-weak` (`#006bd7`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `-apple-system, system-ui, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen-Sans, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (top step `wpds-typography-font-size-2xl`: 32px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, system-ui, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen-Sans, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (`wpds-typography-font-family-body`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Menlo, Consolas, monaco, monospace` (`wpds-typography-font-family-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (9)**: `wpds-typography-font-size-2xl` (32px), `wpds-typography-font-size-xl` (20px), `wpds-typography-font-family-body` (16px), `wpds-typography-font-family-heading` (16px), `wpds-typography-font-family-mono` (16px), `wpds-typography-font-size-lg` (15px), `wpds-typography-font-size-md` (13px), `wpds-typography-font-size-sm` (12px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#fff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#3858e9` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#3858e9` with `#f7f7f8` text (5.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
