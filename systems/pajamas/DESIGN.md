# Pajamas

## Overview

**Pajamas** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#fc6d26` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `GitLab Sans` (36px display down to 16px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `#f7f7f5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Gitlab**. Source: published token package, repo https://github.com/gitlab-org/gitlab-ui, package `@gitlab/ui`, frameworks: vue, css. Inventory: **90** colors, **45** typography steps, and **25** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f7f5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#fc6d26` (2.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#a5a3a3` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `gl-avatar-fallback-text-color-blue` (`#9dc8f2`), `gl-badge-tier-background-color-hover` (`#a997e1`), `gl-badge-tier-icon-color-focus` (`#291a5c`), `brand-purple-01` (`#a989f5`), `gl-action-strong-confirm-background-color-active` (`#3f8edd`), `gl-feedback-brand-background-color` (`#291a5c`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `10` (`#f7f7f5`), `gl-border-color-default` (`#4d4b4e`), `gl-color-brand-gray-04` (`#45424d`), `gl-color-neutral-300` (`#a5a3a3`), `gl-color-neutral-1000` (`#050408`), `gl-badge-muted-border-color-hover` (`#a5a3a3`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `gl-badge-success-background-color-default` (`#35b597`) · danger: `gl-alert-info-border-top-color` (`#3f8edd`) · warning: `gl-color-brand-orange-02-p` (`#fc6d26`) · info: `gl-badge-info-text-color-default` (`#1d2153`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"GitLab Sans"` (top step `display`: clamp(1.75rem, 0.8611111111rem + 1.8518518519vw, 2.25rem), weight 600, line-height 1.125).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"GitLab Sans"` (`font-family-regular`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"GitLab Mono"` (`font-family-monospace`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (45)**: `display` (clamp(1.75rem, 0.8611111111rem + 1.8518518519vw, 2.25rem), w600), `gl-font-size-800` (clamp(1.75rem,0.8611111111rem + 1.8518518519vw,2.25rem)), `1` (clamp(1.5rem, 0.8333333333rem + 1.3888888889vw, 1.875rem), w600), `gl-font-size-700` (clamp(1.5rem,0.8333333333rem + 1.3888888889vw,1.875rem)), `gl-font-size-800-fixed` (1.75rem), `font-size-800-fixed` (1.75rem), `2` (clamp(1.3125rem, 0.8680555556rem + 0.9259259259vw, 1.5625rem), w600), `gl-font-size-600` (clamp(1.3125rem,0.8680555556rem + 0.9259259259vw,1.5625rem)).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 25 component tokens with documented paddings: `.375rem .75rem`, `.75rem 1.25rem`, `9px 13px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f7f5` cards; separate sibling sections using `#a5a3a3` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#f7f7f5`) and `1px solid #a5a3a3` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#fc6d26` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `.25rem` (Buttons `.25rem` · Inputs `.25rem` · Cards `.25rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `.25rem` container.

## Components

- **`btn` (button)**: bg `transparent`, ink `#3a383d`, radius `.25rem`, padding `.375rem .75rem`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **`card` (card)**: bg `#fff`, radius `.25rem`, border `1px solid #dcdbd9`.
- **`table` (nav)**: bg `transparent`, ink `#3a383d`.
- **`btn-info-2` (button)**: bg `#1f73ca`, ink `#fff`, (4.8:1), border `1px solid #1f73ca`.
- **`btn-dark-3` (button)**: bg `#3a383d`, ink `#fff`, (11.6:1), border `1px solid #3a383d`.
- **`btn-link-4` (button)**: ink `#007bff`.
- **`btn-light-5` (button)**: bg `#dcdbd9`, ink `#28272d`, (10.7:1), border `1px solid #dcdbd9`.
- **`table-info-2` (nav)**: bg `#c0d8f0`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#fc6d26` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `GitLab Sans` headings with `GitLab Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f7f5` surface shifts and `#a5a3a3` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
