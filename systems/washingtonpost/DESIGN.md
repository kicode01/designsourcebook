# WPDS

## Overview

**WPDS** is a light-first design system anchored on `rgba(250, 238, 249, 1)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (17.5:1 contrast) and `rgba(254, 140, 108, 1)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (large display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `rgba(250, 238, 249, 1)` -> `rgba(255, 246, 241, 1)` surface layering.

Maintained by **Washington Post**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/washingtonpost/wpds-ui-kit#readme), package `@washingtonpost/wpds-ui-kit`. Inventory: **91** colors, **0** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `rgba(250, 238, 249, 1)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `rgba(254, 140, 108, 1)` with `#0b0b0c` text (8.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgba(255, 246, 241, 1)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
