# InstUI

## Overview

**InstUI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#273540` primary ink (12.6:1 contrast) and `#1D354F` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `LatoWeb` (38px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Instructure**. Source: published token package (https://instructure.github.io/instructure-ui/), package `@instructure/ui-themes`. Inventory: **91** colors, **28** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#FFFFFF` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#273540` — body copy and primary headings (12.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#586874` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1D354F` (12.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 11.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#E8EAEC` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `instui-canvas-colors-ui-text-title` (`#273540`), `instui-canvas-colors-ui-text-timestamp` (`#586874`), `text-description` (`#273540`), `text-author` (`#586874`), `rose30-primary` (`#FB5D5D`), `rose110-primary` (`#7F0000`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `placeholder-background-color` (`#707070`), `instui-canvas-colors-ui-surface-card-primary` (`#FFFFFF`), `instui-canvas-colors-ui-surface-divider` (`#E8EAEC`), `surface-page-primary` (`#FFFFFF`), `surface-card-secondary` (`#F2F4F4`), `text-placeholder` (`#9EA6AD`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#FFFFFF` cards; separate sibling sections using `#E8EAEC` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#FFFFFF`) and `1px solid #E8EAEC` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1D354F` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#273540` primary text and `#586874` secondary labels.
- Use `#1D354F` with `#f7f7f8` text (11.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `LatoWeb` headings with `LatoWeb` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#FFFFFF` surface shifts and `#E8EAEC` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
