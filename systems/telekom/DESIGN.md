# Scale Design System

## Overview

**Scale Design System** is a dark-first design system anchored on `#242426` canvas surfaces with `#ffffff` primary ink (15.5:1 contrast) and `#9e0051` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `TeleNeoWeb` (76px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Deutsche Telekom**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/telekom/design-tokens#readme), package `@telekom/design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **42** typography steps, and **11** documented components (MPL-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#242426` — base application backdrop (dark theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#242426` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#ffffff` — body copy and primary headings (15.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#d91308` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#9e0051` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 7.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #ffffff 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `telekom-color-text-and-icon-on-subtle-blue` (`#2238df`), `telekom-color-text-and-icon-on-subtle-olive` (`#557317`), `telekom-color-primary-pressed` (`#9e0051`), `telekom-color-functional-focus-on-dark-background` (`#7d94f9`), `primary-pressed` (`#A980FF`), `focus-on-dark-background` (`#7d94f9`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `0` (`#FFFFFF`), `telekom-color-text-and-icon-standard` (`#000000`), `telekom-color-text-and-icon-inverted-standard` (`#ffffff`), `telekom-color-background-surface-subtle` (`#efeff0`), `background-surface-subtle` (`#242426`), `surface-highlight` (`#48484b`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `telekom-color-text-and-icon-functional-success` (`#008653`) · danger: `telekom-color-text-and-icon-on-subtle-red` (`#c30a03`) · warning: `telekom-color-functional-warning-pressed` (`#e65703`) · info: `telekom-color-text-and-icon-link-active` (`#000d99`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `TeleNeoWeb, sans-serif` (top step `telekom-typography-font-size-title-1`: 4.75rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `TeleNeoWeb, sans-serif` (`telekom-typography-font-size-body`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `monospace` (`telekom-typography-font-family-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (42)**: `telekom-typography-font-size-title-1` (4.75rem), `tokens` (4.75rem), `typography-font-size-title-1` (4.75rem), `telekom-typography-font-size-title-2` (4.25rem), `typography-font-size-title-2` (4.25rem), `scl-font-size-56` (3.5rem), `telekom-typography-font-size-title-3` (3.375rem), `typography-font-size-title-3` (3.375rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 11 component tokens with documented paddings: `0`, `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#242426` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #ffffff 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#242426` -> `#242426`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #ffffff 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#9e0051` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#242426` (dark) with `#ffffff` primary text and `#d91308` secondary labels.
- Use `#9e0051` with `#f7f7f8` text (7.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `TeleNeoWeb` headings with `TeleNeoWeb` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#242426` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #ffffff 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of dark polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
