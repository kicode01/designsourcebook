# Semrush Intergalactic Design System

## Overview

**Semrush Intergalactic Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#1877f2` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (48px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f4f5f9` surface layering.

Maintained by **Semrush**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/semrush/intergalactic), repo https://github.com/semrush/intergalactic, package `intergalactic`, frameworks: react. Inventory: **91** colors, **8** typography steps, and **16** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f4f5f9` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1877f2` (4.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.0:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `brand-facebook-value` (`#1877f2`), `brand-google-blue-value` (`#1a0dab`), `brand-google-my-business-value` (`#1a73e8`), `brand-instagram-value` (`#e4405f`), `brand-pinterest-value` (`#bd081c`), `brand-twitter-value` (`#1d9bf0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `50` (`#f4f5f9`), `200` (`#c4c7cf`), `400` (`#8a8e9b`), `600` (`#484a54`), `800` (`#191b23`), `neutral` (`rgba(30, 34, 49, 1)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success` (`rgba(68, 209, 164, 1)`) · danger: `tag-primary-red-hover-active-value` (`#ffbdbf`) · warning: `warning` (`rgba(160, 13, 42, 1)`) · info: `linkedin` (`#1a7ab2`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (top step `size-800`: 48px, weight 700, line-height 1.17).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` (`size-300`: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.5).
- **Scale Steps (8)**: `size-800` (48px, w700), `size-700` (36px, w700), `size-600` (32px, w700), `size-500` (24px, w700), `size-400` (20px, w500), `size-300` (16px, w400), `size-200` (14px, w400), `size-100` (12px, w400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f4f5f9` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f4f5f9`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1877f2` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Intergalactic Icons (`@semcore/icon`) on a `16x16` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#1877f2` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.0:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f4f5f9` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
