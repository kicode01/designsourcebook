# AT UIKIT

## Overview

**AT UIKIT** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFF` canvas surfaces with `#3F536E` primary ink (7.9:1 contrast) and `#6190E8` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (32px display down to 14px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **AT**. Source: published token package, package `at-ui-style`. Inventory: **91** colors, **37** typography steps, and **37** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F7F7F7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#3F536E` — body copy and primary headings (7.9:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#3F536E` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#6190E8` (3.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#CCC` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primary-color` (`#6190E8`), `brand-color` (`#6190E8`), `brand-color-light` (`#78A4F4`), `brand-color-dark` (`#346FC2`), `color-primary` (`#6190E8`), `brand-blue-100` (`#D0DEF8`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `brand-blue-50` (`#ECF2FC`), `border-color-gray` (`#CCC`), `color-white` (`#FFF`), `green-50` (`#E3F9ED`), `red-50` (`#FFE9E9`), `blue-50` (`#EFF4FE`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success-color` (`#13CE66`) · danger: `error-color` (`#FF4949`) · warning: `warning-color` (`#FFC82C`) · info: `info-color` (`#78A4FA`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 37 component tokens with documented paddings: `6px 16px`, `1px 8px`, `8px 16px`, `12px 16px`, `0 6px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F7F7F7` cards; separate sibling sections using `#CCC` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 1px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`) paired with `#CCC` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#6190E8` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `4px` · Cards `4px` · Badges `4px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFF` (light) with `#3F536E` primary text and `#3F536E` secondary labels.
- Use `#6190E8` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F7F7F7` surface shifts and `#CCC` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
