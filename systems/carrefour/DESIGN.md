# Marcel

## Overview

**Marcel** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FFFFFF` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0970E6` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Ubuntu` with `DM sans` body copy (72px display down to 13px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Carrefour**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://carrefour.design/). Inventory: **54** colors, **13** typography steps, and **30** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FFFFFF` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0970E6` (4.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#E5E5E5` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `colors-blocks-upload-background-annotation` (`#0970E6`), `colors-blocks-rules-background-do` (`#B9E9CC`), `colors-nav-text-active` (`#0970E6`), `colors-nav-text-main` (`#1D345C`), `colors-blocks-tabs-border-active` (`#0970E6`), `colors-nav-border-active` (`#0970E6`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `colors-blocks-callout-background-second` (`#E8F8FF`), `colors-blocks-callout-background-fourth` (`#FFEFEF`), `colors-blocks-callout-background-third` (`#F0FAF6`), `colors-blocks-upload-text-annotation` (`#ffffff`), `colors-footer-border-divider` (`#E5E5E5`), `colors-blocks-rules-background-neutral` (`#F0F3F6`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 30 component tokens with documented paddings: `2px`, `12px 25px`, `8px 12px`, `10px 14px`, `6px 10px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#E5E5E5` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0 10px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.1)`, `0 0 10px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.1)`) paired with `#E5E5E5` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0970E6` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `5px` · Inputs `4px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FFFFFF` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0970E6` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Ubuntu` headings with `DM sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#E5E5E5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
