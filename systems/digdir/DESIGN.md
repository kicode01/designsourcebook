# Designsystemet

## Overview

**Designsystemet** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#eeb04c` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Norwegian Digitalisation Agency**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/digdir/designsystemet/tree/main/packages/theme), package `@digdir/designsystemet-theme`. Inventory: **91** colors, **1** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#eeb04c` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#bbbcbd` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-accent-surface-hover` (`#c7ddf0`), `color-accent-border-subtle` (`#99c0e3`), `color-accent-border-strong` (`#005db1`), `color-accent-text-default` (`#002c54`), `color-accent-base-hover` (`#004f96`), `color-accent-base-contrast-subtle` (`#dbe9f5`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-accent-background-default` (`#ffffff`), `color-accent-surface-default` (`#ffffff`), `color-neutral-background-default` (`#ffffff`), `color-neutral-surface-default` (`#ffffff`), `color-neutral-surface-hover` (`#d9dadb`), `color-neutral-border-subtle` (`#bbbcbd`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-success-surface-hover` (`#c3e2c7`) · danger: `color-danger-surface-hover` (`#f3d2d2`) · warning: `color-warning-surface-hover` (`#f6d5a0`) · info: `color-info-surface-hover` (`#c5ddf0`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter` (top step `font-family`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter` (`font-family`: 16px, weight 400).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#bbbcbd` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid #bbbcbd` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#eeb04c` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#eeb04c` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#bbbcbd` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
