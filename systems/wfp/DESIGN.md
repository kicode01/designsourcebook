# WFP UI Kit

## Overview

**WFP UI Kit** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#001f2f` primary ink (17.0:1 contrast) and `#007dbc` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (64px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f0f3f6` surface layering.

Maintained by **World Food Programme**. Source: published token package, repo https://github.com/wfp/designsystem, package `@wfp/ui`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **29** typography steps, and **16** documented components (Apache-2 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f0f3f6` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#001f2f` — body copy and primary headings (17.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#5a6872` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#007dbc` (4.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #001f2f 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-dark-text-01` (`#031c2d`), `accent-02` (`#f7b825`), `brand-01` (`#007dbc`), `hover-primary` (`#005e8d`), `accent-01` (`#f7b825`), `text-01` (`#001f2f`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `text-02` (`#5a6872`), `text-03` (`#dfe6eb`), `disabled-01` (`#f9fbfc`), `disabled-02` (`#dfe6eb`), `disabled` (`#f9fbfc`), `color-gray-1` (`#dfe3e6`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-dark-green` (`#336d37`) · danger: `red-hex` (`#c5192d`) · warning: `yellow-hex` (`#fcc30b`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Open Sans", sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 4rem, weight 700).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Open Sans", sans-serif` (`base-font-size`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (29)**: `h1` (4rem, w700), `wfp-story-title` (3.375rem, w300), `wfp-story-content-title` (2.25rem, w300), `wfp-photo-card-info-title` (1.75rem), `wfp-modal-header-heading` (1.75rem), `wfp-secondary-navigation-title` (1.75rem, w400), `wfp-form-group-title` (1.25rem, w600), `calendar-month-caption` (18px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f0f3f6` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #001f2f 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f0f3f6`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #001f2f 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#007dbc` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: WFP UI Kit Icons (`@wfp/icons`) on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#001f2f` primary text and `#5a6872` secondary labels.
- Use `#007dbc` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f0f3f6` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #001f2f 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
