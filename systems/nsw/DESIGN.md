# Digital NSW

## Overview

**Digital NSW** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#002664` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Public Sans` (48px display down to 16px body), unified by `0.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **State of New South Wales**. Source: published token package (https://www.digital.nsw.gov.au/digital-design-system), package `nsw-design-system`. Inventory: **91** colors, **24** typography steps, and **35** documented components (ISC license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f2f2f2` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#002664` (14.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 13.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `nsw-brand-dark` (`#002664`), `nsw-brand-light` (`#cbedfd`), `nsw-brand-supplementary` (`#146cfd`), `nsw-brand-accent` (`#d7153a`), `nsw-brand-accent-light` (`#ffe6ea`), `nsw-brand-dark-rgb` (`rgb(0, 38, 100)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `success-bg` (`#e5f6e6`), `info-bg` (`#eaedf4`), `warning-bg` (`#fbeee5`), `error-bg` (`#f7e7eb`), `nsw-text-dark` (`#22272b`), `nsw-text-light` (`#fff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `success` (`#008a07`) · danger: `error` (`#b81237`) · warning: `warning` (`#c95000`) · info: `info` (`#2e5299`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Public Sans", sans-serif` (top step `nsw-font-size-xxl-desktop`: 3rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Public Sans", sans-serif` (`nsw-base-font-size`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (24)**: `nsw-font-size-xxl-desktop` (3rem), `h1` (2.75rem), `nsw-font-size-xxl-mobile` (2.25rem), `h2` (2.25rem), `nsw-font-size-xl-desktop` (2rem), `nsw-font-size-xl-mobile` (1.75rem), `h3` (1.75rem), `nsw-font-size-lg-desktop` (1.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 35 component tokens with documented paddings: `.125rem 1rem`, `0 1rem`, `.5rem`, `1.5rem 0`, `1rem 1rem 3.875rem`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f2f2f2` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0.25rem 0.75rem 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`, `0 4px 9px -5px rgba(0,0,0,.4)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#002664` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0.25rem` (Buttons `0.25rem` · Cards `0.25rem` · Badges `1.5rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `0.25rem` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#002664` with `#f7f7f8` text (13.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Public Sans` headings with `Public Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `0.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (0.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f2f2f2` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `0.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
