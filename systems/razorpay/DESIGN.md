# Blade

## Overview

**Blade** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f5f5f5` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.0:1 contrast) and `hsla(218, 89%, 51%, 1)` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Inter` with `System UI` body copy (40px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and stepped `#f5f5f5` -> `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` surface layering.

Maintained by **Razorpay**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/razorpay/blade), repo https://github.com/razorpay/blade. Inventory: **90** colors, **22** typography steps, and **13** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f5f5f5` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `hsla(218, 89%, 51%, 1)` (4.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `hsla(204, 8%, 88%, 1)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `surface-background-sea-intense` (`hsla(180, 61%, 20%, 1)`), `surface-text-on-cloud-subtle` (`hsla(218, 87%, 43%, 1)`), `surface-icon-on-sea-subtle` (`hsla(155, 100%, 31%, 1)`), `interactive-border-primary-highlighted` (`hsla(218, 87%, 43%, 1)`), `interactive-text-primary-muted` (`hsla(218, 100%, 63%, 1)`), `interactive-icon-primary-subtle` (`hsla(218, 89%, 51%, 1)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `background-page` (`#f5f5f5`), `surface-background-gray-moderate` (`hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)`), `surface-border-gray-subtle` (`hsla(204, 8%, 88%, 1)`), `surface-text-gray-muted` (`hsla(204, 9%, 42%, 1)`), `surface-icon-gray-normal` (`hsla(0, 0%, 2%, 1)`), `surface-icon-static-white-normal` (`hsla(0, 0%, 100%, 1)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `feedback-text-positive-subtle` (`hsla(149, 38%, 86%, 1)`) · danger: `feedback-border-negative-intense` (`hsla(4, 85%, 36%, 1)`) · warning: `data-background-categorical-orange-moderate` (`hsla(24, 100%, 71%, 1)`) · info: `feedback-background-notice-intense` (`hsla(25, 100%, 44%, 1)`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter, "Inter Fallback Arial", Arial` (top step `font-size-1100`: 40px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Fira Sans", "Droid Sans", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (`body`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Menlo, "San Francisco Mono", "Courier New", "Roboto Mono", monospace` (`font-family-code`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (22)**: `font-size-1100` (40px), `font-size-1000` (38px), `font-size-900` (36px), `font-size-800` (34px), `font-size-700` (32px), `font-size-600` (24px), `h2` (24px, w600), `font-size-500` (20px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 13 component tokens with documented paddings: `12px 20px`, `0`, `16px`, `1px 3px`, `8px 12px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` cards; separate sibling sections using `hsla(204, 8%, 88%, 1)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f5f5f5` -> `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)`) and `1px solid hsla(204, 8%, 88%, 1)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `hsla(218, 89%, 51%, 1)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `12px` · Cards `4px` · Badges `3px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f5f5f5` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `hsla(218, 89%, 51%, 1)` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `hsla(0, 0%, 97%, 1)` surface shifts and `hsla(204, 8%, 88%, 1)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
