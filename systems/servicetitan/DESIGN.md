# Anvil

## Overview

**Anvil** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#ff8670` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Nunito Sans` (40px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#fcfcfc` surface layering.

Maintained by **ServiceTitan**. Source: published token package, package `@servicetitan/anvil2`. Inventory: **91** colors, **17** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see package license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fcfcfc` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#ff8670` (2.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 8.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `chart-status-neutral-stroke-color` (`#8c9ca5`), `a2-chart-status-neutral-stroke-color` (`#8c9ca5`), `color-blue-grey-800` (`#24333d`), `color-cyan-700` (`#075563`), `color-magenta-600` (`#be288d`), `color-lime-500` (`#6b9013`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-neutral-0` (`#ffffff`), `color-neutral-10` (`#fcfcfc`), `color-neutral-20` (`#f9f9f9`), `color-neutral-30` (`#f6f6f6`), `color-neutral-40` (`#f1f1f1`), `color-neutral-50` (`#eeeeee`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `chart-status-success-fill-color` (`#007a4d`) · danger: `chart-status-danger-fill-color` (`#e13212`) · warning: `chart-status-warning-fill-color` (`#ffbe00`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Nunito Sans", sans-serif` (top step `font-size`: 40px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Nunito Sans", sans-serif` (`a2-font-family-base`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (17)**: `font-size` (40px), `a2-font-size-900` (2.25rem), `a2-font-size-800` (2rem), `a2-font-size-700` (1.75rem), `a2-font-size-600` (1.5rem), `a2-font-size-500` (1.25rem), `a2-font-size-400` (1rem), `a2-typography-paragraph-font-family` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fcfcfc` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#fcfcfc`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#ff8670` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#ff8670` with `#0b0b0c` text (8.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Nunito Sans` headings with `Nunito Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fcfcfc` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
