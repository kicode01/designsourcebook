# dotUI

## Overview

**dotUI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#e31c5f` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Helvetica Now Text` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `.5rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#e31c5f` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Helvetica Now Text` headings with `Helvetica Now Text` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.5rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.5rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f5f5f5` surface shifts and `#e5e5e5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.5rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
