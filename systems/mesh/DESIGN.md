# Mesh Design System

## Overview

**Mesh Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#144a38` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Crimson Pro` with `Buenos Aires` body copy (60px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Mesh**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://www.meshdesignsystem.com/), frameworks: css. Inventory: **75** colors, **30** typography steps, and **16** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#144a38` (10.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 9.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#9e9e9e` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `theme-color-bg` (`#f7e1a8`), `theme-color-bg-hover` (`#fbf0d4`), `theme-color-fg-brand` (`#144a38`), `theme-color-fg-selected` (`#13362a`), `theme-color-fg-label` (`#144a38`), `theme-color-border` (`#eaaf18`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `common-color-white` (`#ffffff`), `common-color-black` (`#000000`), `theme-color-fg` (`#1b1b1b`), `theme-color-fg-prominent` (`#111111`), `theme-color-fg-gentle` (`#474747`), `theme-color-fg-muted` (`#5e5e5e`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `theme-color-fg-success` (`#144a38`) · danger: `theme-color-fg-error` (`#7a0c0c`) · warning: `theme-color-fg-warning` (`#302405`) · info: `theme-color-fg-info` (`#003b77`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Crimson Pro", "Iowan Old Style", "Sitka Display", "Palatino Linotype", Charter, Palatino, serif` (top step `theme-typography-heading2xl-font-size`: 3.75rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Buenos Aires", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`theme-typography-copy-font-size`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (30)**: `theme-typography-heading2xl-font-size` (3.75rem), `theme-typography-heading-xl-font-size` (2.25rem), `h1` (2em), `theme-typography-heading-lg-font-size` (1.5rem), `theme-typography-heading-md-font-size` (1.25rem), `theme-typography-copy-large-font-size` (1.125rem), `button-typography-large-font-size` (1.125rem), `theme-typography-copy-font-size` (1rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#9e9e9e` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid #9e9e9e` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#144a38` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#144a38` with `#f7f7f8` text (9.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Crimson Pro` headings with `Buenos Aires` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#9e9e9e` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
