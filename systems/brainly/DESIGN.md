# Pencil

## Overview

**Pencil** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#000000` primary ink (21.0:1 contrast) and `#1877f2` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Figtree` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `20px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Brainly**. Source: published token package, package `brainly-style-guide`. Inventory: **91** colors, **6** typography steps, and **37** documented components (GPL-3.0-only license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ebf2f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#000000` — body copy and primary headings (21.0:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#014a82` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1877f2` (4.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.0:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#dbe4ea` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `text-blue-60` (`#014a82`), `text-blue-40` (`#4fb3f6`), `text-indigo-60` (`#133191`), `text-indigo-40` (`#6d83f3`), `text-gray-60` (`#46535f`), `text-gray-50` (`#687b8c`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `text-black` (`#000000`), `text-white` (`#ffffff`), `text-gray-70` (`#323c45`), `focus-inner-color` (`#ffffff`), `active-border-color` (`#dbe4ea`), `hover-background-color` (`#dbe4ea`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `text-green-60` (`#00672e`) · danger: `text-red-60` (`#cf1d00`) · warning: `text-yellow-60` (`#935000`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Figtree, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 2em, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Figtree, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`body`: 1rem, weight 400, line-height 1.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 37 component tokens with documented paddings: `0 16px`, `0 20px`, `6px 16px`, `0`, `0 36px 0 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ebf2f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `#dbe4ea` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 8px 32px 0 rgba(50,60,69,.2)`, `0 2px 8px 0 rgba(50,60,69,.2)`) paired with `#dbe4ea` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1877f2` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `20px` (Buttons `20px` · Inputs `20px` · Cards `8px` · Badges `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#000000` primary text and `#014a82` secondary labels.
- Use `#1877f2` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.0:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Figtree` headings with `Figtree` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `20px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ebf2f7` surface shifts and `#dbe4ea` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `20px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
