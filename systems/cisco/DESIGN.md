# Momentum Design

## Overview

**Momentum Design** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#91BFFF` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Momentum` (52px display down to 20px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Cisco**. Source: published token package, package `@momentum-design/tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **62** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see package license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#91BFFF` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `mds-color-theme-text-accent-hover` (`#bfe3ff`), `mds-color-theme-text-team-cyan-hover` (`#22c7d6`), `mds-color-theme-text-team-lime-hover` (`#93c437`), `mds-color-theme-text-team-purple-hover` (`#f294f1`), `mds-color-theme-text-team-slate-hover` (`#b0b4d9`), `mds-color-theme-background-label-gold-normal` (`#30240d`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `0` (`#17181b`), `100` (`#ffffff`), `mds-color-theme-surface-neutral-0-default` (`#17181b`), `mds-color-theme-bg-primary` (`#000000`), `mds-color-theme-background-solid-primary-normal` (`#000000`), `mds-color-theme-background-alert-default-normal` (`#3b3b3b`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `mds-color-theme-text-success-active` (`#9fedd8`) · danger: `mds-color-theme-text-alert-orange-hover` (`#fed5ac`) · warning: `mds-color-theme-content-text-warning-default` (`#f2990a`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Momentum, Inter, Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` (top step `mds-font-size-headline-small`: 3.25rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Momentum, Inter, Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` (`mds-font-apps-heading-small-regular-font-size`: 1.25rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (62)**: `mds-font-size-headline-small` (3.25rem), `mds-font-apps-headline-small-light-font-size` (3.25rem), `mds-font-apps-headline-small-regular-font-size` (3.25rem), `regular` (52px), `font-size` (52px), `small` (52px), `mds-font-size-heading-xlarge` (2.5rem), `mds-font-apps-heading-xlarge-regular-font-size` (2.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#91BFFF` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#91BFFF` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Momentum` headings with `Momentum` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
