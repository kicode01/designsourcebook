# Telefónica Mística

## Overview

**Telefónica Mística** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0066ff` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (64px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f7f7ff` surface layering.

Maintained by **Telefónica**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/Telefonica/mistica-web), repo https://github.com/Telefonica/mistica-web, package `@telefonica/mistica`, frameworks: react, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **38** typography steps, and **16** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f7ff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0066ff` (4.8:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `mistica-blau-blau-blue-primary` (`#00b6f1`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-secondary` (`#0072bc`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-primary30` (`#b3e9fb`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-secondary60` (`#005a99`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-secondary20` (`#b2d4ec`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-secondary30` (`#80b7df`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `mistica-blau-blau-blue-primary20` (`#e5f6fd`), `mistica-blau-blau-blue-secondary10` (`#e5f1f9`), `mistica-cyber-brand10` (`#f7f7ff`), `mistica-vivo-evolution-vivo-neutral0` (`#ffffff`), `mistica-vivo-evolution-vivo-neutral25` (`#f1eff6`), `mistica-vivo-evolution-vivo-neutral150` (`#e3deeb`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `vivo-green950` (`#143824`) · danger: `blau-red` (`#D12937`) · warning: `mistica-telefonica-yellow` (`#e4c35c`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `-apple-system, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `mistica-font-size-10`: 4rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`mistica-font-size-card-pretitle-default`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (38)**: `mistica-font-size-10` (4rem), `mistica-font-size-9` (3.5rem), `mistica-font-size-8` (3rem), `mistica-font-size-7` (2.5rem), `mistica-font-size-6` (2rem), `mistica-font-size-title4` (2rem), `mistica-font-size-feedback-screen-title` (2rem), `mistica-font-size-5` (1.75rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f7ff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f7f7ff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0066ff` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0066ff` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f7ff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
