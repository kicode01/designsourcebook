# Decathlon Vitamin

## Overview

**Decathlon Vitamin** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0082c3` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Roboto` (96px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fff` -> `#eff1f3` surface layering.

Maintained by **Decathlon**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/Decathlon/vitamin-web/tree/main/packages/sources/css/src/design-tokens), repo https://github.com/Decathlon/vitamin-web, package `@vtmn/css-design-tokens`, frameworks: css, react, vue, svelte. Inventory: **42** colors, **15** typography steps, and **16** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#eff1f3` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0082c3` (4.2:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `vtmn-color-brand` (`#0082c3`), `vtmn-color-brand-pro` (`#012b49`), `vtmn-color-brand-digital` (`#007dbc`), `vtmn-color-brand-digital-dark-1` (`#00689d`), `vtmn-color-brand-digital-dark-2` (`#00537d`), `vtmn-color-brand-digital-light-1` (`#7ebee0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `vtmn-color-black` (`#001018`), `vtmn-color-brand-digital-light-3` (`#e7f3f9`), `vtmn-color-green-light-3` (`#e4f9f3`), `vtmn-color-grey-light-1` (`#b3bac3`), `vtmn-color-grey-light-2` (`#d9dde1`), `vtmn-color-grey-light-3` (`#eff1f3`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `vtmn-color-green` (`#02be8a`) · danger: `vtmn-color-red` (`#ee000c`) · warning: `vtmn-color-orange` (`#fe5800`) · info: `vtmn-color-info` (`#007dbc`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Roboto, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` (top step `vtmn-typo-display-1-font-size`: 6rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Roboto, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` (`vtmn-typo-font-family`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (15)**: `vtmn-typo-display-1-font-size` (6rem), `vtmn-typo-display-2-font-size` (3.75rem), `vtmn-typo-title-1-font-size` (3rem), `vtmn-modal-content-title-text` (2.6rem), `vtmn-typo-title-2-font-size` (2.5rem), `vtmn-typo-title-3-font-size` (2rem), `vtmn-typo-title-4-font-size` (1.625rem), `vtmn-alert-content-title` (1.6rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#eff1f3` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fff` -> `#eff1f3`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0082c3` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Decathlon Vitamin Icons (`@vtmn/icons`) on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0082c3` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Roboto` headings with `Roboto` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#eff1f3` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
