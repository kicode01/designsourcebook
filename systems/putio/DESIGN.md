# Put.io Design System

## Overview

**Put.io Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.5:1 contrast) and `hsl(46, 100%, 27%)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `GT America` (96px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Put.io**. Source: published token package (https://design.put.io), package `@putdotio/design`. Inventory: **91** colors, **19** typography steps, and **9** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `hsl(46, 100%, 27%)` (4.8:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `hsl(0, 0%, 88.7%)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `button-primary-bg-hover` (`hsl(45, 89%, 58%)`), `primary-foreground` (`hsl(38, 65%, 10%)`), `badge-background` (`hsl(358, 70%, 45%)`), `brand-text` (`hsl(43, 32%, 21%)`), `context-tv-channel-art-nightly-moon` (`hsl(44.7, 97.9%, 63.1%)`), `surface-light-transfer-list-item-downloading-bg` (`hsl(85, 76.0%, 92.3%)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `bg-secondary` (`hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)`), `input-border-hover` (`hsl(0, 0%, 78.0%)`), `field-border-focus` (`hsl(0, 0%, 52.3%)`), `accent-foreground` (`hsl(0, 0%, 9.0%)`), `alert-info-text` (`hsl(0, 0%, 9.0%)`), `slider-fill-disabled` (`hsl(0, 0%, 43.5%)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `notification-success-border` (`hsl(140, 39%, 68%)`) · danger: `button-danger-bg` (`hsl(358, 75%, 59%)`) · warning: `yellow-text` (`hsl(43, 32%, 21%)`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"GT America", sans-serif` (top step `original-value`: 6rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"GT America", sans-serif` (`typography-font-size-base`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Berkeley Mono", monospace` (`mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (19)**: `original-value` (6rem), `typography-font-size-display` (6rem), `typography-font-size-3xl` (4rem), `typography-font-size-4xl` (4rem), `typography-font-size-2xl` (3rem), `typography-font-size-xl` (2rem), `typography-font-size-lg` (1.5rem), `typography-font-size-title` (1.375rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 9 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` cards; separate sibling sections using `hsl(0, 0%, 88.7%)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` -> `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)`) and `1px solid hsl(0, 0%, 88.7%)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `hsl(46, 100%, 27%)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Phosphor Icons (`@phosphor-icons/react`) on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `hsl(46, 100%, 27%)` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `GT America` headings with `GT America` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%)` surface shifts and `hsl(0, 0%, 88.7%)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
