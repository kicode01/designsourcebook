# Calcite Design System

## Overview

**Calcite Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#009af2` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System Sans` (64px display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f7f7f7` surface layering.

Maintained by **Esri**. Source: published token package, package `@esri/calcite-design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **51** typography steps, and **8** documented components (SEE LICENSE.md license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f7f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#009af2` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `calcite-color-surface-highlight` (`#2b465f`), `calcite-color-text-highlight` (`#d6efff`), `calcite-color-brand` (`#009af2`), `calcite-color-brand-hover` (`#40b9ff`), `calcite-color-brand-press` (`#80d1ff`), `primary` (`#009af2`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `calcite-color-neutral-blk-000` (`#ffffff`), `calcite-color-neutral-blk-005` (`#f7f7f7`), `calcite-color-neutral-blk-010` (`#f2f2f2`), `calcite-color-neutral-blk-020` (`#ebebeb`), `calcite-color-neutral-blk-030` (`#dedede`), `calcite-color-neutral-blk-040` (`#d4d4d4`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `calcite-color-status-success` (`#36da43`) · danger: `calcite-color-status-danger` (`#fe583e`) · warning: `calcite-color-status-warning` (`#f89927`) · info: `calcite-color-text-link` (`#00a0ff`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `sans-serif` (top step `calcite-font-size-relative-7xl`: 4rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `sans-serif` (`calcite-font-size-relative-base`: 0.875rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Andale Mono"` (`calcite-font-family-andale-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (51)**: `calcite-font-size-relative-7xl` (4rem), `calcite-font-size-relative7xl` (4rem), `calcite-font-size-relative-6xl` (3.5rem), `calcite-font-size-relative6xl` (3.5rem), `calcite-font-size-relative-5xl` (3rem), `calcite-font-size-relative5xl` (3rem), `calcite-font-size-relative-4xl` (2.5rem), `calcite-font-size-relative4xl` (2.5rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f7f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f7f7f7`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#009af2` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#009af2` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System Sans` headings with `System Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f7f7` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
