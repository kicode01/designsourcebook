# Feelix

## Overview

**Feelix** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0969da` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Mona Sans VF` (32px display down to 14px body), unified by `6px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **MYOB**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (http://feelix.myob.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **16** typography steps, and **31** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0969da` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Mona Sans VF` headings with `Mona Sans VF` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `6px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (6px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `#d1d9e0` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `6px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `prc-components-Input-IwWrt` fields and secondary buttons.
