# PIE Design System

## Overview

**PIE Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fdfdfb` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.3:1 contrast) and `#f36805` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Arial` with `JetSansDigital` body copy (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fdfdfb` -> `#f6f3ef` surface layering.

Maintained by **Just**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/justeattakeaway/pie/packages/tools/pie-design-tokens#readme), package `@justeat/pie-design-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **36** typography steps, and **8** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fdfdfb` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f6f3ef` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.3:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#f36805` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#f0ece6` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `border-selected-brand` (`#f36805`), `border-decorative-brand` (`#ff8000`), `interactive-brand` (`#f36805`), `support-brand-01` (`#ff8000`), `support-brand-02` (`#fbc592`), `support-brand-tonal` (`#fddfc3`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `background-default` (`#fdfdfb`), `background-subtle` (`#f6f3ef`), `background-dark` (`#1c1a17`), `border-default` (`#f0ece6`), `border-subtle` (`#f6f3ef`), `border-strong` (`#e7e2da`).
- **Semantic States**: danger: `interactive-error` (`#cc0300`) · warning: `support-warning` (`#ffd600`) · info: `support-info` (`#3147ad`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Arial` (top step `family-secondary`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `JetSansDigital` (`dt-font-body-s-family`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `PTMono` (`dt-font-family-code`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (36)**: `family-secondary` (16px), `dt-font-family-alternative` (16px), `dt-font-family-code` (16px), `dt-font-family-display` (16px), `dt-font-family-primary` (16px), `dt-font-family-secondary` (16px), `dt-font-heading-xs-family` (16px), `dt-font-heading-s-family` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f6f3ef` cards; separate sibling sections using `#f0ece6` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fdfdfb` -> `#f6f3ef`) and `1px solid #f0ece6` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#f36805` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fdfdfb` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#f36805` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Arial` headings with `JetSansDigital` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f6f3ef` surface shifts and `#f0ece6` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
