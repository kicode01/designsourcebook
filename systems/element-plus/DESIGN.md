# Element Plus

## Overview

**Element Plus** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fafcff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.1:1 contrast) and `rgb(64, 158, 255)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (64px display down to 16px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Source: published token package (https://element-plus.org/), package `element-plus`. Inventory: **90** colors, **41** typography steps, and **37** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fafcff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ecf5ff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.1:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `rgb(64, 158, 255)` (2.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e4e7ed` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `el-color-primary-rgb` (`rgb(64, 158, 255)`), `el-color-primary` (`#409eff`), `el-color-primary-light-3` (`#79bbff`), `el-color-primary-light-5` (`#a0cfff`), `el-color-primary-light-7` (`#c6e2ff`), `el-color-primary-light-8` (`#d9ecff`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fafcff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `rgb(64, 158, 255)` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ecf5ff` surface shifts and `#e4e7ed` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-otp` fields and secondary buttons.
