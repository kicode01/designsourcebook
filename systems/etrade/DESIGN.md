# E-Trade Design Language

## Overview

**E-Trade Design Language** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#1773b0` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Inter` (30px display down to 14px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **E-Trade**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (http://etrade.design/). Inventory: **91** colors, **8** typography steps, and **22** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1773b0` (5.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e2e2e2` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-interactive-primary-default` (`#1773b0`), `color-interactive-primary-hover` (`#005c90`), `color-interactive-primary-active` (`#002451`), `color-interactive-primary-focus` (`#1773b0`), `color-interactive-secondary-focus` (`#1773b0`), `campfire-docs-light-color-interactive-primary-defaul` (`#1773b0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-text-default` (`#242424`), `color-text-muted` (`#666666`), `color-text-reverse` (`#ffffff`), `color-border-container` (`#e2e2e2`), `color-border-divider` (`#e2e2e2`), `color-border-form` (`#666666`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-text-positive` (`#107a1b`) · danger: `color-text-negative` (`#cc0000`) · warning: `color-status-warning` (`#bb831b`) · info: `color-status-info` (`#1773b0`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter, sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 30px, weight 400, line-height 40px).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Inter, sans-serif` (`body`: .875rem, weight 400, line-height 1.5).
- **Scale Steps (8)**: `h1` (30px, w400), `h6` (20px, w400), `h2` (20px, w600), `h3` (1.25rem, w500), `html` (100%), `h4` (1rem, w500), `body` (.875rem, w400), `h5` (.875rem, w500).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 22 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px`, `30px`, `2px 3px`, `1px`, `0`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#e2e2e2` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 1px 7px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.13)`) paired with `#e2e2e2` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1773b0` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0px` (Buttons `50%` · Inputs `0` · Badges `999px`).
- **Strict Sharp Edges**: Maintain `0px` architectural corners across buttons, inputs, and cards.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `square` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#1773b0` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Inter` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#e2e2e2` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-group-addon` fields and secondary buttons.
