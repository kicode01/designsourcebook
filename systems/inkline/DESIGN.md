# Inkline

## Overview

**Inkline** is a light-first design system anchored on `hsla(0 0% 100% / 1)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `hsla(19.622641509433947 92.9824561403509% 66.47058823529412% / 1)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Source: published token package (https://github.com/uxfront-com/inkline), repo https://github.com/uxfront-com/inkline. Inventory: **62** colors, **22** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `hsla(0 0% 100% / 1)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `hsla(19.622641509433947 92.9824561403509% 66.47058823529412% / 1)` with `#0b0b0c` text (8.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `hsla(0 0% 100% / 1)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
