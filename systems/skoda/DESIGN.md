# Flow

## Overview

**Flow** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#6acb75` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `SKODA Next` (64px display down to 20px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Skoda**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://flow.skoda-brand.com/d/6aQMrmD6PhWL). Inventory: **91** colors, **117** typography steps, and **37** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#6acb75` (2.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `rgb(216, 216, 216)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `rc-focus-color` (`#08f`), `color-charts-primary-default` (`#6acb75`), `color-charts-secondary-default` (`#d59bff`), `f-theme-settings-button-primary-background-color-hov` (`rgb(168, 255, 204)`), `color-focus-default` (`#4196fb`), `fc-secondary-base-color` (`#d59bff`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#6acb75` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `SKODA Next` headings with `SKODA Next` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `rgb(216, 216, 216)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
