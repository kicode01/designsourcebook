# Stacks

## Overview

**Stacks** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#FF5E00` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Stack Overflow**. Source: published token package, repo https://github.com/StackExchange/Stacks, package `@stackoverflow/stacks`, frameworks: css. Inventory: **91** colors, **3** typography steps, and **16** documented components (see package license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#FF5E00` (3.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `brand` (`#FF5E00`), `brand-blue-light` (`#C6D1E1`), `brand-blue` (`#5074EF`), `brand-blue-dark` (`#00165E`), `brand-brown-light` (`#998B7A`), `brand-pink` (`#F39FFF`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `brand-black` (`#201C1D`), `brand-off-white` (`#F0EFEE`), `white` (`#ffffff`), `black-050` (`#ffffff`), `black-100` (`#f7f6f5`), `black-150` (`#f0efed`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `brand-green` (`#86AF25`) · danger: `red-200` (`#f5b9b8`) · warning: `brand-orange-medium` (`#6E1527`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI Adjusted", "Segoe UI", "Liberation Sans", sans-serif` (top step `h1`: 2em, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI Adjusted", "Segoe UI", "Liberation Sans", sans-serif` (`theme-body-font-family`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (3)**: `h1` (2em), `theme-body-font-family` (16px), `html` (100%).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#FF5E00` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#FF5E00` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
