# Elisa Design System

## Overview

**Elisa Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#F6F7F8` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.3:1 contrast) and `#2323FF` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Jost Medium` with `DM sans` body copy (72px display down to 13px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Elisa**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://designsystem.elisa.fi/). Inventory: **71** colors, **13** typography steps, and **30** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#F6F7F8` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F6F7F8` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.3:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#2323FF` (7.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 7.1:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#DBE1E6` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `colors-blocks-callout-background-fourth` (`#FFD9DB`), `colors-blocks-callout-background-first` (`#FFE6B4`), `colors-blocks-upload-background-annotation` (`#212185`), `colors-blocks-upload-text-label` (`#090933`), `colors-ui-element-text` (`#090933`), `colors-ui-element-background-action` (`#2323FF`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `colors-blocks-rules-background-caution` (`#FFF7E9`), `colors-blocks-rules-background-do` (`#E9F9F2`), `colors-blocks-rules-background-neutral` (`#F6F7F8`), `colors-blocks-status-tags-background-greyscale` (`#ECEEF0`), `colors-blocks-status-tags-background-neutral` (`#E4EAFC`), `colors-blocks-callout-background-third` (`#E9F9F2`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `colors-blocks-status-tags-background-positive` (`#ACE5D1`) · danger: `colors-blocks-status-tags-background-negative` (`#FFD9DB`) · warning: `colors-blocks-status-tags-background-warning` (`#FFE6B4`) · info: `colors-footer-text-links` (`#2323FF`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Jost Medium"` (top step `landing-title`: 4.5rem, weight 300).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"DM sans", "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen-Sans, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (`body`: 0.8125rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (13)**: `landing-title` (4.5rem, w300), `landing-subtitle` (3rem, w300), `type-landing-heading` (2.5rem, w800), `landing-bottom-cta-title` (2.4rem, w400), `h1` (2rem), `big-title` (2rem, w250), `type-heading-large` (1.5rem, w700), `h3` (1.3rem, w700).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 30 component tokens with documented paddings: `2px`, `12px 25px`, `8px 12px`, `10px 14px`, `6px 10px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F6F7F8` cards; separate sibling sections using `#DBE1E6` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0 10px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.1)`, `0 0 10px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.1)`) paired with `#DBE1E6` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#2323FF` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Buttons `5px` · Inputs `4px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#F6F7F8` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#2323FF` with `#f7f7f8` text (7.1:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Jost Medium` headings with `DM sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F6F7F8` surface shifts and `#DBE1E6` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
