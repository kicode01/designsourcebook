# UIkit

## Overview

**UIkit** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#1e87f0` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `System UI` with `Consolas` body copy (240px display down to 16px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **YOOtheme**. Source: published token package (https://getuikit.com), package `uikit`. Inventory: **22** colors, **41** typography steps, and **40** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8f8f8` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1e87f0` (3.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e5e5e5` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `global-primary-background` (`#1e87f0`), `base-ins-background` (`#ffd`), `base-mark-background` (`#ffd`), `base-selection-background` (`#39f`), `table-row-active-background` (`#ffd`), `primary` (`#1e87f0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `global-emphasis-color` (`#333`), `global-color` (`#666`), `global-inverse-color` (`#fff`), `global-muted-background` (`#f8f8f8`), `global-muted-color` (`#999`), `global-background` (`#fff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `global-success-background` (`#32d296`) · danger: `global-danger-background` (`#f0506e`) · warning: `global-warning-background` (`#faa05a`) · info: `global-link-color` (`#1e87f0`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#1e87f0` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `Consolas` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#f8f8f8` surface shifts and `#e5e5e5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
