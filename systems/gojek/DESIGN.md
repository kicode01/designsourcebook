# Asphalt

## Overview

**Asphalt** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#0d6efd` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (120px display down to 16px body), unified by `.25rem` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **GO-JEK**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://asphalt.gojek.io/). Inventory: **80** colors, **10** typography steps, and **40** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#0d6efd` (4.5:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e8e8e8` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `bs-blue` (`#0d6efd`), `bs-indigo` (`#6610f2`), `bs-purple` (`#6f42c1`), `bs-pink` (`#d63384`), `bs-teal` (`#20c997`), `bs-cyan` (`#0dcaf0`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `bs-white` (`#fff`), `bs-gray` (`#6c757d`), `bs-gray-dark` (`#343a40`), `bs-gray-100` (`#f8f9fa`), `bs-gray-200` (`#e9ecef`), `bs-gray-300` (`#dee2e6`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `bs-green` (`#198754`) · danger: `bs-red` (`#dc3545`) · warning: `bs-orange` (`#fd7e14`) · info: `bs-info` (`#0dcaf0`).

- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#e8e8e8` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 .5rem 1rem rgba(0,0,0,.15)`, `0px 0px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`) paired with `#e8e8e8` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#0d6efd` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `.25rem` (Buttons `.25rem` · Cards `.25rem` · Badges `.25rem`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#0d6efd` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `.25rem` base geometry across inputs and buttons (.25rem on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `#e8e8e8` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `.25rem` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `form-check-input` fields and secondary buttons.
