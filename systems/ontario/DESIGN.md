# Ontario Design System

## Overview

**Ontario Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#42c7ff` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#f2f2f2` surface layering.

Maintained by **Ontario**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://designsystem.ontario.ca/docs/documentation/release-notes.html#latest), repo https://github.com/ongov/ontario-design-system, package `@ongov/ontario-design-system-global-styles`, frameworks: web-components, css. Inventory: **91** colors, **1** typography steps, and **16** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f2f2f2` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#42c7ff` (1.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `nav-text` (`#0066cc`), `css-surface-10` (`#cba52e`), `css-surface-11` (`#fcaf17`), `css-surface-12` (`#c1b28f`), `css-surface-13` (`#39b54a`), `css-surface-14` (`#8dc63f`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `canvas-text` (`#191919`), `css-surface-5` (`#f2f2f2`), `css-surface-6` (`#cccccc`), `css-surface-7` (`#999999`), `css-surface-8` (`#666666`), `css-surface-9` (`#4c4c4c`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Open Sans", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `nav`: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Open Sans", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`nav`: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 16 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f2f2f2` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#f2f2f2`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#42c7ff` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#42c7ff` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f2f2f2` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
