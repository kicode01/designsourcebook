# Scania Digital Design System

## Overview

**Scania Digital Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#16417f` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `scania sans` (216px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` -> `rgba(243, 244, 247, 1)` surface layering.

Maintained by **Scania**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/scania-digital-design-system/tegel), repo https://github.com/scania-digital-design-system/tegel, package `@scania/tegel`, frameworks: web-components, react, angular. Inventory: **91** colors, **65** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgba(243, 244, 247, 1)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#16417f` (10.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 9.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `900` (`#001533`), `scania-neutral-solid-800` (`rgba(58, 69, 84, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-700` (`rgba(83, 95, 112, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-600` (`rgba(117, 131, 152, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-500` (`rgba(147, 161, 183, 1)`), `spinner-background` (`rgba(0, 72, 82, 1)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `50` (`#D8DDE5`), `component-cookie-color-text-primary-inverse` (`#fff`), `scania-neutral-solid-1000` (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-958` (`rgba(14, 16, 19, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-928` (`rgba(21, 24, 29, 1)`), `scania-neutral-solid-900` (`rgba(28, 34, 43, 1)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `green-600` (`#004c3b`) · danger: `red-800` (`#480008`) · warning: `warning` (`#f1c21b`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"scania sans"` (top step `super-type-01-font-size`: 216px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"scania sans"` (`scania-font-family-default`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (65)**: `super-type-01-font-size` (216px), `super-type-02-font-size` (156px), `super-type-03-font-size` (112px), `expressive-headline-01-font-size` (80px), `expressive-headline-02-font-size` (56px), `headline-01-font-size` (40px), `headline-02-font-size` (32px), `headline-03-font-size` (24px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `10px 18px`, `9px 13px`, `18px 20px`, `4px 10px`, `10px 16px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgba(243, 244, 247, 1)` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` -> `rgba(243, 244, 247, 1)`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#16417f` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Buttons `8px` · Inputs `8px` · Cards `12px` · Badges `999px` · Navigation `8px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `12px` container.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#16417f` with `#f7f7f8` text (9.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `scania sans` headings with `scania sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgba(243, 244, 247, 1)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input-default` fields and secondary buttons.
