# Swarm Design System

## Overview

**Swarm Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `rgb(255, 255, 255)` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `rgb(0, 212, 128)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Graphik Meetup` (42px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `rgb(255, 255, 255)` -> `#ffffff` surface layering.

Maintained by **Meetup**. Source: published token package, package `swarm-constants`. Inventory: **81** colors, **11** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `rgb(255, 255, 255)` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `rgb(0, 212, 128)` (2.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 10.1:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `c-pink` (`rgb(255, 153, 209)`), `c-light-blue` (`rgb(77, 209, 237)`), `c-purple` (`rgb(55, 30, 172)`), `c-blue` (`rgb(0, 162, 199)`), `c-plum` (`rgb(112, 0, 176)`), `c-teal` (`rgb(0, 212, 128)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `rgba` (`rgb(255, 255, 255)`), `hex` (`#ffffff`), `hsl` (`hsl(0, 0%, 100%)`), `android-hex8` (`#ffffffff`), `c-white` (`rgb(255, 255, 255)`), `c-light-gray` (`rgb(246, 247, 248)`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `c-success` (`rgb(0, 212, 128)`) · danger: `c-red` (`rgb(241, 58, 89)`) · warning: `c-yellow` (`rgb(255, 229, 51)`) · info: `c-linkedin` (`rgb(72, 117, 180)`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Graphik Meetup", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (top step `font-size-display1`: 42px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Graphik Meetup", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`font-size-normal`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Monaco, "Andale Mono", "Courier New", monospace` (`font-family-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (11)**: `font-size-display1` (42px), `font-size-display2` (34px), `font-size-title` (32px), `font-size-display3` (28px), `font-size-big` (24px), `font-size-section-title` (20px), `font-size-normal` (16px), `font-family-normal` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`rgb(255, 255, 255)` -> `#ffffff`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `rgb(0, 212, 128)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `rgb(255, 255, 255)` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `rgb(0, 212, 128)` with `#0b0b0c` text (10.1:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Graphik Meetup` headings with `Graphik Meetup` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
