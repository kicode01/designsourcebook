# uStyle

## Overview

**uStyle** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fcfdfd` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.3:1 contrast) and `#4cc0f4` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (32px display down to 16px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **uSwitch**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://ustyle.guide/). Inventory: **88** colors, **21** typography steps, and **41** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fcfdfd` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f6f6f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.3:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#4cc0f4` (2.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#a7a7ac` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `css-surface-5` (`#ffff00`), `css-border-9` (`#d64226`), `css-border-10` (`#2aaa5b`), `css-fill-17` (`#3c5a99`), `css-fill-18` (`#dc4e41`), `css-fill-19` (`#ffd32f`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `canvas-text` (`#141424`), `h1-text` (`#ffffff`), `link-bg` (`#f4f5f6`), `link-text` (`#65717b`), `css-color-6` (`#000000`), `css-border-7` (`#a7a7ac`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 41 component tokens with documented paddings: `0px`, `0.35em 0.5em`, `0px 1em 0.5em 0px`, `0.35em 40px 0.35em 0.5em`, `10px 26px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f6f6f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `#a7a7ac` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0px 2px 2px 0px rgba(204, 204, 204, 0.6)`) paired with `#a7a7ac` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#4cc0f4` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fcfdfd` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#4cc0f4` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#f6f6f7` surface shifts and `#a7a7ac` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `us-form-input` fields and secondary buttons.
