# Sirio

## Overview

**Sirio** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#13a5b9` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Titillium Web` (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#ffffff` -> `#F1F2F4` surface layering.

Maintained by **INPS**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/INPS-it/sirio-design-token), repo https://github.com/INPS-it/sirio-design-token. Inventory: **91** colors, **7** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F1F2F4` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#13a5b9` (3.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 2.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-global-primary-100` (`#2f6dd5`), `color-global-accent-110` (`#13a5b9`), `color-global-dark-primary-115` (`#002460`), `color-global-mid-primary-70` (`#84a9e6`), `color-global-mid-secondary-80` (`#727f8d`), `color-alias-interactive-accent-default` (`#18cee7`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-global-secondary-90` (`#5b6772`), `color-global-light-primary-40` (`#f2f6fc`), `color-global-dark-secondary-130` (`#000000`), `color-global-dark-secondary-110` (`#2d3339`), `color-global-light-secondary-50` (`#c6ccd2`), `color-alias-interactive-secondary-default` (`#454d56`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-global-semantic-success-100` (`#008069`) · danger: `color-alias-text-color-alert` (`#aa224f`) · warning: `color-global-semantic-warning-90` (`#f97c1d`) · info: `semantic-info-100` (`#2F6DD5`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Titillium Web"` (top step `typography-global-font-family-01`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Titillium Web"` (`typography-global-font-family-01`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Roboto Mono"` (`typography-global-font-family-03`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `typography-global-font-family-01` (16px), `typography-global-font-family-02` (16px), `typography-global-font-family-03` (16px), `typography-alias-font-family-heading-01` (16px), `typography-alias-font-family-text-01` (16px), `typography-alias-font-family-text-02` (16px), `typography-alias-font-family-number-03` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F1F2F4` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#ffffff` -> `#F1F2F4`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#13a5b9` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#13a5b9` with `#f7f7f8` text (2.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Titillium Web` headings with `Titillium Web` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F1F2F4` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
