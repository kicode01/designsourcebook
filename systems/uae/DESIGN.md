# UAE Design System

## Overview

**UAE Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fcfcfc` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.2:1 contrast) and `#422ad5` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `Inter` with `Roboto` body copy (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fcfcfc` -> `#f9f7ed` surface layering.

Maintained by **UAE**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/TDRA-ae/aegov-dls), repo https://github.com/TDRA-ae/aegov-dls. Inventory: **91** colors, **8** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fcfcfc` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f9f7ed` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.2:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#422ad5` (8.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 7.8:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primary-default` (`#422ad5`), `secondary-default` (`#f43098`), `color-aegold-50` (`#361e12`), `color-aegold-200` (`#6c4527`), `color-aegold-400` (`#92722a`), `color-aegold-600` (`#cba344`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `color-aegold-950` (`#f9f7ed`), `color-aeblack-100` (`#101828`), `color-aeblack-500` (`#6a7282`), `color-aeblack-700` (`#d1d5dc`), `color-aeblack-900` (`#f1f5f9`), `color-whitely-50` (`#0e0f12`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `color-aegreen-200` (`#cae8cf`) · danger: `color-aered-100` (`#fde4e3`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Inter, "Helvetica Neue", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` (top step `font-heading`: 16px, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Roboto, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (`font-body`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (8)**: `font-heading` (16px), `font-body` (16px), `text-h1-font-family` (16px), `text-h2-font-family` (16px), `text-h3-font-family` (16px), `text-h4-font-family` (16px), `text-h5-font-family` (16px), `text-h6-font-family` (16px).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f9f7ed` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fcfcfc` -> `#f9f7ed`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#422ad5` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fcfcfc` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#422ad5` with `#f7f7f8` text (7.8:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Inter` headings with `Roboto` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f9f7ed` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
