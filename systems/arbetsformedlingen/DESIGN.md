# Arbetsformedlingen Design System

## Overview

**Arbetsformedlingen Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fefefe` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.5:1 contrast) and `#1db920` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (40px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#fefefe` -> `#F1F7E5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Arbetsformedlingen**. Source: published token package (https://designsystem.arbetsformedlingen.se/), package `@designsystem-se/af`. Inventory: **91** colors, **40** typography steps, and **8** documented components (Apache-2.0 license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fefefe` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F1F7E5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#1db920` (2.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 7.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#d5d5d5` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `digi-color-logotype-secondary-symbol-light-af18` (`#1db920`), `digi-color-logotype-primary-symbol-dark` (`#1db920`), `digi-color-logotype-secondary-symbol-dark` (`#1db920`), `digi-color-logotype-secondary-symbol-light` (`#1db920`), `color-forest-700` (`#02703C`), `color-jade-100` (`#BDDFDA`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `sentiment-informative-200` (`#E9E9FF`), `sentiment-error-200` (`#FDEDED`), `sentiment-success-200` (`#E9F6EC`), `digi-progress-circle-border-color` (`#d5d5d5`), `digi-sentiment-error-200` (`#fdeded`), `digi-sentiment-informative-200` (`#e9e9ff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `sentiment-success-500` (`#009322`) · danger: `sentiment-error-500` (`#DA0000`) · warning: `sentiment-warning-200` (`#FFF398`) · info: `sentiment-informative-500` (`#0000FF`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Open Sans"` (top step `global-typography-font-size-largest-6`: 2.5rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Open Sans"` (`global-typography-font-size-base`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `Consolas, Monaco, "Andale Mono", "Ubuntu Mono", monospace` (`global-typography-font-family-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (40)**: `global-typography-font-size-largest-6` (2.5rem), `digi-global-typography-font-size-largest-6` (2.5rem), `global-typography-font-size-largest-5` (2.25rem), `digi-global-typography-font-size-largest-5` (2.25rem), `global-typography-font-size-largest-4` (2rem), `digi-global-typography-font-size-largest-4` (2rem), `global-typography-font-size-largest-3` (1.75rem), `digi-global-typography-font-size-largest-3` (1.75rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F1F7E5` cards; separate sibling sections using `#d5d5d5` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#fefefe` -> `#F1F7E5`) and `1px solid #d5d5d5` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#1db920` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fefefe` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#1db920` with `#0b0b0c` text (7.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F1F7E5` surface shifts and `#d5d5d5` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
