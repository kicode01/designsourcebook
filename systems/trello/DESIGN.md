# Nachos

## Overview

**Nachos** is a light-first design system anchored on `#E9F2FE` canvas surfaces with `#5D1F1A` primary ink (11.1:1 contrast) and `#144794` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `normal 653 2rem/2.25rem Atlassian Sans` with `normal 400 1rem/1.5rem Atlassian Sans` body copy (16px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Trello**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://design.trello.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **16** typography steps, and **8** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#E9F2FE` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#E9F2FE` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#5D1F1A` — body copy and primary headings (11.1:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#164B35` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#144794` (7.9:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 8.3:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#FFFFFF` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `background-accent-lime-subtlest` (`#EFFFD6`), `background-accent-lime-subtle` (`#94C748`), `background-accent-teal-subtle` (`#6CC3E0`), `background-accent-blue-subtle` (`#669DF1`), `background-accent-purple-subtle` (`#C97CF4`), `background-accent-magenta-subtle` (`#E774BB`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `background-accent-red-subtlest` (`#FFECEB`), `background-accent-teal-subtlest` (`#E7F9FF`), `background-accent-blue-subtlest` (`#E9F2FE`), `background-accent-purple-subtlest` (`#F8EEFE`), `background-accent-magenta-subtlest` (`#FFECF8`), `background-accent-gray-subtlest` (`#F0F1F2`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `background-accent-green-subtlest` (`#DCFFF1`) · danger: `background-accent-red-subtle` (`#F87168`) · warning: `background-accent-orange-subtlest` (`#FFF5DB`) · info: `border-information-subtle` (`#8FB8F6`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#E9F2FE` cards; separate sibling sections using `#FFFFFF` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#E9F2FE` -> `#E9F2FE`) and `1px solid #FFFFFF` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#144794` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#E9F2FE` (light) with `#5D1F1A` primary text and `#164B35` secondary labels.
- Use `#144794` with `#f7f7f8` text (8.3:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `normal 653 2rem/2.25rem Atlassian Sans` headings with `normal 400 1rem/1.5rem Atlassian Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#E9F2FE` surface shifts and `#FFFFFF` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
