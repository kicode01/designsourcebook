# Ionic Framework

## Overview

**Ionic Framework** is a dark-first design system anchored on `#000` canvas surfaces with `#f7f7f8` primary ink (19.6:1 contrast) and `#4d8dff` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Roboto` (26px display down to 16px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and hairline `1px` structural borders.

Maintained by **Ionic**. Source: published token package (https://ionicframework.com/), package `@ionic/core`. Inventory: **91** colors, **14** typography steps, and **17** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#000` — base application backdrop (dark theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#000` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels (separated via `1px` border lines).
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#f7f7f8` — body copy and primary headings (19.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#f7f7f8` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#4d8dff` (6.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 3.0:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#222222` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `ion-color-primary` (`#4d8dff`), `ion-color-primary-rgb` (`rgb(77, 141, 255)`), `ion-color-primary-shade` (`#447ce0`), `ion-color-primary-tint` (`#5f98ff`), `ion-color-secondary` (`#46b1ff`), `ion-color-secondary-rgb` (`rgb(70, 177, 255)`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#000` (dark) with `#f7f7f8` primary text and `#f7f7f8` secondary labels.
- Use `#4d8dff` with `#f7f7f8` text (3.0:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Roboto` headings with `Roboto` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#000` surface shifts and `#222222` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of dark polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
