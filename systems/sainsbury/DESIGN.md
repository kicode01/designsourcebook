# Luna

## Overview

**Luna** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f2f2f2` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (17.6:1 contrast) and `#135ca3` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `MaryAnn` with `Arial` body copy (60px display down to 18px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#f2f2f2` -> `#fff` surface layering.

Maintained by **Sainsbury**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://design-systems.sainsburys.co.uk/). Inventory: **91** colors, **63** typography steps, and **12** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f2f2f2` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (17.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#135ca3` (6.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 6.4:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#d8d8d8` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `color-alias-toggletip-hover-border` (`#f06c00`), `color-alias-cta-primary-default-background` (`#f06c00`), `color-alias-cta-secondary-hover-text` (`#e55000`), `color-palette-brand-1-highlight` (`#135ca3`), `color-alias-cta-secondary-hover-border` (`#e55000`), `color-palette-brand-1-dark` (`#e55000`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 12 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#fff` cards; separate sibling sections using `#d8d8d8` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f2f2f2` -> `#fff`) and `1px solid #d8d8d8` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#135ca3` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px` (Badges `2px`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f2f2f2` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#135ca3` with `#f7f7f8` text (6.4:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `MaryAnn` headings with `Arial` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fff` surface shifts and `#d8d8d8` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
