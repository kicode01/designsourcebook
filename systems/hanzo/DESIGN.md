# Hanzo Design System

## Overview

**Hanzo Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#FAFAFA` canvas surfaces with `#737373` primary ink (4.5:1 contrast) and `#f59e0b` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Zen` (112px display down to 14px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#FAFAFA` -> `#F5F5F5` surface layering.

Maintained by **Hanzo AI**. Source: published token package, package `@hanzo/tokens`. Inventory: **90** colors, **17** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#FAFAFA` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#F5F5F5` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#737373` — body copy and primary headings (4.5:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#737373` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#f59e0b` (2.1:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 9.2:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #737373 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primary` (`#f59e0b`), `data-ruby-3` (`#3a141e`), `data-ruby-10` (`#ec5a72`), `data-crimson-5` (`#5c1839`), `data-crimson-12` (`#fdd3e8`), `data-tomato-7` (`#863a2d`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `neutral-50` (`#FAFAFA`), `neutral-100` (`#F5F5F5`), `neutral-200` (`#E5E5E5`), `neutral-300` (`#D4D4D4`), `neutral-400` (`#A3A3A3`), `neutral-500` (`#737373`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `state-success` (`#22c55e`) · danger: `state-error` (`#ef4444`) · warning: `state-warning` (`#f59e0b`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `Zen, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` (top step `font-size-9xl`: 7rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Zen, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` (`font-size-base`: 0.875rem, weight 400).
- **Monospace / Technical Labels**: `"Zen Mono", ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", "Courier New", monospace` (`font-mono`: 16px).
- **Scale Steps (17)**: `font-size-9xl` (7rem), `font-size-8xl` (5.25rem), `font-size-7xl` (4rem), `font-size-6xl` (3.25rem), `font-size-5xl` (2.5rem), `font-size-4xl` (2rem), `font-size-3xl` (1.625rem), `font-size-2xl` (1.3125rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#F5F5F5` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #737373 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#FAFAFA` -> `#F5F5F5`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #737373 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#f59e0b` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#FAFAFA` (light) with `#737373` primary text and `#737373` secondary labels.
- Use `#f59e0b` with `#0b0b0c` text (9.2:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Zen` headings with `Zen` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#F5F5F5` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #737373 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
