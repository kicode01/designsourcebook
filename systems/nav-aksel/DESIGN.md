# Aksel

## Overview

**Aksel** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f5f6f7` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.2:1 contrast) and `ax-accent-500` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Source Sans 3` (40px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#f5f6f7` -> `#f8eaef` surface layering.

Maintained by **NAV Norway**. Source: published token package (https://aksel.nav.no/grunnleggende/styling/design-tokens/), package `@navikt/ds-tokens`. Inventory: **91** colors, **11** typography steps, and **8** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f5f6f7` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f8eaef` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.2:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `ax-accent-500` (distinct vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for verified contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `ax-accent-200` (`#e4eeff`), `ax-accent-300` (`#d4e5fd`), `ax-accent-400` (`#bad5fb`), `ax-accent-500` (`#428ae3`), `ax-accent-600` (`#2176d4`), `ax-accent-700` (`#0063c1`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `ax-neutral-100` (`#f5f6f7`), `ax-neutral-200` (`#ecedef`), `ax-neutral-300` (`#e1e3e7`), `ax-neutral-400` (`#cfd3d8`), `ax-neutral-500` (`#818997`), `ax-neutral-600` (`#6f7785`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `ax-success-100` (`#e2fde8`) · danger: `ax-danger-300` (`#ffd9e6`) · warning: `ax-warning-100` (`#fff5e4`) · info: `ax-info-400` (`#c0d6e4`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `"Source Sans 3", "Source Sans Pro", Arial, sans-serif` (top step `ax-font-size-heading-2xlarge`: 2.5rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `"Source Sans 3", "Source Sans Pro", Arial, sans-serif` (`ax-font-size-medium`: 1rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (11)**: `ax-font-size-heading-2xlarge` (2.5rem), `ax-font-size-heading-xlarge` (2rem), `ax-font-size-heading-large` (1.75rem), `ax-font-size-heading-medium` (1.5rem), `ax-font-size-heading-small` (1.25rem), `ax-font-size-xlarge` (1.25rem), `ax-font-size-heading-xsmall` (1.125rem), `ax-font-size-large` (1.125rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f8eaef` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f5f6f7` -> `#f8eaef`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `ax-accent-500` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Inline System SVGs on a `20x20` grid (outline, stroke `1.75px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f5f6f7` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `ax-accent-500` with `#f7f7f8` text (verified) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Source Sans 3` headings with `Source Sans 3` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f8eaef` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
