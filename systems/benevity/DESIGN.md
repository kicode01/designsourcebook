# Skyline

## Overview

**Skyline** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#de175c` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Open Sans` (41px display down to 14px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Benevity**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://skyline.benevity.org/). Inventory: **16** colors, **50** typography steps, and **50** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#fafafa` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#de175c` (4.8:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.5:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e0e0e0` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `sidebar-color` (`#001a61`), `header-background-color` (`#001a61`), `button-bg` (`#de175c`), `button-border-1` (`#c21954`), `primary` (`#de175c`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#de175c` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.5:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Open Sans` headings with `Open Sans` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#fafafa` surface shifts and `#e0e0e0` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `sky-date-input` fields and secondary buttons.
