# Visa Product Design System

## Overview

**Visa Product Design System** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#fcc015` brand accent.

Display headlines pair `visa-font-semi-bold` with `visa-font-regular` body copy (69px display down to 18px body), unified by `0px` razor-sharp architectural corners and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Visa**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://design.visa.com/welcome/). Inventory: **45** colors, **50** typography steps, and **20** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#ffffff` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#fcc015` (1.7:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 11.9:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `v-button-default-foreground` (`#bdeaff`), `v-button-default-focus-foreground` (`#80d7ff`), `palette-default-active-pressed` (`#80d7ff`), `palette-default-active` (`#1434cb`), `palette-default-active-hover` (`#173be8`), `palette-default-active-accent` (`#fcc015`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `v-button-tertiary-hover-background` (`#4c4c4c`), `v-badge-background` (`#fff`), `v-badge-foreground` (`#000`), `v-divider-high-contrast-decorative` (`#000000`), `v-logo-hc-light-foreground` (`#000`), `v-logo-hc-dark-foreground` (`#fff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `palette-messaging-surface-positive` (`#d6f2c4`) · danger: `palette-messaging-surface-negative` (`#ffd6e9`) · warning: `palette-messaging-highlight-warning` (`#ffef99`) · info: `palette-messaging-highlight-info` (`#c7edff`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `visa-font-semi-bold, "Open Sans", sans-serif` (top step `typography-display-1-font-size`: 4.2857142857rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `visa-font-regular, "Open Sans", sans-serif` (`typography-body-1-font-size`: 1.1428571429rem, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (50)**: `typography-display-1-font-size` (4.2857142857rem), `typography-display-2-font-size` (3.5rem), `typography-headline-1-font-size` (2.2857142857rem), `v-input-font-size` (2.143rem), `typography-headline-2-font-size` (1.7857142857rem), `typography-headline-3-font-size` (1.4285714286rem), `typography-headline-4-font-size` (1.2857142857rem), `typography-subtitle-1-font-size` (1.1428571429rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 20 component tokens.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#ffffff` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, .2)`, `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, .2),inset 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0)`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#fcc015` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `0px` (Buttons `0px` · Inputs `0px` · Navigation `0px`).
- **Strict Sharp Edges**: Maintain `0px` architectural corners across buttons, inputs, and cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#fcc015` with `#0b0b0c` text (11.9:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `visa-font-semi-bold` headings with `visa-font-regular` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Enforce `0px` border-radius consistently across cards, inputs, and primary buttons.
- Separate containers via `#ffffff` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not apply rounded corners (> 0px) to structural containers or CTA buttons.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
