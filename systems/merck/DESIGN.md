# Liquid

## Overview

**Liquid** is a dark-first design system anchored on `rgb(0, 0, 0)` canvas surfaces with `#f7f7f8` primary ink (19.6:1 contrast) and `rgb(255, 179, 184)` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Lato` (92px display down to 18px body), unified by `4px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Merck**. Source: published token package, package `@emdgroup-liquid/liquid`. Inventory: **91** colors, **41** typography steps, and **11** documented components (SEE LICENSE IN LICENSE.md license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `rgb(0, 0, 0)` — base application backdrop (dark theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgb(43, 43, 59)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#f7f7f8` — body copy and primary headings (19.6:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#f7f7f8` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `rgb(255, 179, 184)` (12.4:1 vs canvas; pair with `#0b0b0c` foreground ink for 11.6:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `neutral-900` (`rgb(15, 26, 46)`), `ld-col-neutral-900` (`#0f1a2e`), `rr-200` (`rgb(255, 179, 184)`), `rr-500` (`rgb(244, 58, 102)`), `rr-800` (`rgb(117, 12, 36)`), `rb-300` (`rgb(129, 187, 233)`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `neutral-010` (`rgb(248, 248, 252)`), `neutral-050` (`rgb(236, 236, 244)`), `neutral-100` (`rgb(225, 225, 234)`), `neutral-200` (`rgb(199, 199, 209)`), `neutral-300` (`rgb(169, 169, 186)`), `neutral-400` (`rgb(139, 139, 156)`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 11 component tokens with documented paddings: `1.5rem`, `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgb(43, 43, 59)` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 0.0625rem 0.25rem #0f1a2e26,0 0 0.0625rem #0f1a2e26`) paired with `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `rgb(255, 179, 184)` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `4px` (Badges `250rem`).
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `4px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `2px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `rgb(0, 0, 0)` (dark) with `#f7f7f8` primary text and `#f7f7f8` secondary labels.
- Use `rgb(255, 179, 184)` with `#0b0b0c` text (11.6:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Lato` headings with `Lato` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `4px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (4px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgb(43, 43, 59)` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #f7f7f8 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of dark polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `4px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
