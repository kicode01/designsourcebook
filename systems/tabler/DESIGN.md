# Tabler UI

## Overview

**Tabler UI** is a light-first design system anchored on `#fff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#066fd1` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (80px display down to 16px body), unified by `12px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Tabler**. Source: published token package (https://tabler.io), package `@tabler/core`. Inventory: **91** colors, **109** typography steps, and **25** documented components (MIT license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#fff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `rgb(231, 240, 249)` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `rgba(153, 159, 164, 1)` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#066fd1` (5.0:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 4.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `rgb(72.836712561%, 72.836712561%, 72.836712561%)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `tblr-primary` (`#066fd1`), `tblr-brand` (`#066fd1`), `text-color` (`#7a96a0`), `tblr-gray-400` (`#94a3b8`), `tblr-gray-600` (`#475569`), `tblr-gray-800` (`#1e293b`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `text-secondary-dark` (`rgba(153, 159, 164, 1)`), `tblr-secondary` (`#737373`), `tblr-linkedin-lt-rgb` (`rgb(231, 240, 249)`), `tblr-table-border-color` (`rgb(72.836712561%, 72.836712561%, 72.836712561%)`), `tblr-dropdown-link-color` (`#d4d4d4`), `tblr-dropdown-link-hover-color` (`#fff`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `tblr-success` (`#2fb344`) · danger: `tblr-danger` (`#d63939`) · warning: `tblr-warning` (`#f59f00`) · info: `linkedin` (`#0a66c2`).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 25 component tokens with documented paddings: `0.5rem 1rem`, `0.25rem 0.5rem`, `0`, `0.5625rem 1rem`, `2px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `rgb(231, 240, 249)` cards; separate sibling sections using `rgb(72.836712561%, 72.836712561%, 72.836712561%)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Layered Shadow Model**: Uses documented shadows (`0 1px 2px 0 color-mix(in oklab, light-dark(rgba(18, 18, 23, 0.4), #000) 12.5%, transparent)`) paired with `rgb(72.836712561%, 72.836712561%, 72.836712561%)` borders for elevated overlays.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `#066fd1` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `12px`.

- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `30x30` grid (solid, stroke `0px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#fff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `rgba(153, 159, 164, 1)` secondary labels.
- Use `#066fd1` with `#f7f7f8` text (4.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `12px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (12px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `rgb(231, 240, 249)` surface shifts and `rgb(72.836712561%, 72.836712561%, 72.836712561%)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `12px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
