# GEL

## Overview

**GEL** is a light-first design system anchored on `#f2f9f2` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (18.4:1 contrast) and `primitives-color-wbc-primary-400` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `System UI` (18px display down to 16px body), unified by `8px` base corner radius and stepped `#f2f9f2` -> `#f7f7f7` surface layering.

Maintained by **Westpac**. Source: published token package (https://github.com/WestpacGEL/GEL-next), repo https://github.com/WestpacGEL/GEL-next. Inventory: **91** colors, **7** typography steps, and **8** documented components (see repository license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#f2f9f2` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#f7f7f7` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (18.4:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `primitives-color-wbc-primary-400` (distinct vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for verified contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `primitives-color-wbc-primary-100` (`#facccb`), `primitives-color-wbc-primary-400` (`#ee221b`), `primitives-color-wbc-primary-700` (`#99100b`), `primitives-color-wbc-primary-950` (`#370604`), `primitives-color-stg-primary-200` (`#f87272`), `primitives-color-stg-primary-500` (`#e30000`).
- **Neutral Ramp**: `50` (`#f2f9f2`), `primitives-color-success-50` (`#f2f9f2`), `primitives-color-danger-50` (`#fcf2f2`), `primitives-color-bom-primary-50` (`#fff4f3`), `reserved-info-50` (`#f2f8fc`), `bom-primary-50` (`#fff4f3`).
- **Semantic States**: success: `primitives-color-success-300` (`#47a447`) · danger: `primitives-color-danger-300` (`#db6363`) · warning: `primitives-color-warning-200` (`#e8b199`) · info: `primitives-color-info-100` (`#d9eaf6`).

## Typography

- **Primary / Display Voice**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji", "Segoe UI Symbol"` (top step `form-control-font-size-xlarge`: 1.125rem, weight 600).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji", "Segoe UI Symbol"` (`font-family-sans`: 16px, weight 400).
- **Scale Steps (7)**: `form-control-font-size-xlarge` (1.125rem), `font-family-sans` (16px), `font-sans` (16px), `font-brand` (16px), `form-control-font-size-medium` (1rem), `form-control-font-size-large` (1rem), `form-control-font-size-small` (0.875rem).

## Layout

- **Grid & Control Spacing**: Derived from 8 component tokens with documented paddings: `8px 12px`, `2px`, `6px 12px`, `12px 14px`, `10px 14px`.
- **Hierarchy**: Group controls cleanly within `#f7f7f7` cards; separate sibling sections using `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines rather than oversized whitespace.

## Elevation

- **Surface & Stroke Elevation**: Relies on crisp tonal contrast (`#f2f9f2` -> `#f7f7f7`) and `1px solid color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines instead of heavy drop shadows.
- **Interactive Lift**: Reserve higher surface brightness or focus outlines anchored on `primitives-color-wbc-primary-400` for keyboard focus and active selection states.

## Shapes

- **Base Corner Geometry**: `8px`.
- **Nested Concentricity**: Keep nested pills/badges pill-shaped (`999px`) or tighter than their parent `8px` container.
- **Iconography & SVGs**: Repository SVGs on a `24x24` grid (outline, stroke `1.5px`, `round` caps).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#f2f9f2` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `primitives-color-wbc-primary-400` with `#f7f7f8` text (verified) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `System UI` headings with `System UI` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `8px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (8px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#f7f7f7` surface shifts and `color-mix(in srgb, #0b0b0c 14%, transparent)` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `8px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `input` fields and secondary buttons.
