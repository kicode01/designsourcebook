# Druids

## Overview

**Druids** is a light-first design system anchored on `#ffffff` canvas surfaces with `#0b0b0c` primary ink (19.7:1 contrast) and `#451481` brand accent.

Typography centers cleanly on `Roboto Mono` (68px display down to 14px body), unified by `6px` base corner radius and subtle shadow elevation.

Maintained by **Datadog**. Source: live stylesheet extraction (https://druids.datadoghq.com/). Inventory: **91** colors, **11** typography steps, and **11** documented components (all rights reserved license).

## Colors

- **Canvas (`canvas`)**: `#ffffff` — base application backdrop (light theme).
- **Surface (`surface`)**: `#eaf6fc` — elevated cards, popovers, and panels.
- **Primary Ink (`text`)**: `#0b0b0c` — body copy and primary headings (19.7:1 on canvas).
- **Muted Ink (`muted`)**: `#0b0b0c` — secondary captions, placeholders, and helper labels.
- **Brand Accent (`brand`)**: `#451481` (12.6:1 vs canvas; pair with `#f7f7f8` foreground ink for 11.7:1 contrast).
- **Structural Border (`border`)**: `#e2e5ed` — divider rules, input strokes, and card outlines.
- **Brand & Accent Scale**: `ui-interaction-primary` (`#a4cef7`), `button-inline-shortcut-primary-default-background` (`#09245b`), `popover-background-color` (`#a33bff`), `search-modal-item-is-selected-background` (`#006bc2`), `search-modal-item-is-selected-badge-background` (`#4e91d1`), `search-modal-item-is-selected-background-active` (`#005da9`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Anchor base views on `#ffffff` (light) with `#0b0b0c` primary text and `#0b0b0c` secondary labels.
- Use `#451481` with `#f7f7f8` text (11.7:1) for primary CTAs, active tabs, and focus outlines.
- Pair `Roboto Mono` headings with `Roboto Mono` body copy using documented weights and scale steps.
- Apply `6px` base geometry across inputs and buttons (6px on outer containers).
- Separate containers via `#eaf6fc` surface shifts and `#e2e5ed` hairlines cleanly.

### Don't
- Do not invert the base canvas out of light polarity without explicitly switching component text tokens.
- Do not place low-contrast muted ink or raw white text on light accents without verifying >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not invent arbitrary brand colors, unverified fonts, or synthetic heavy shadows outside the palette above.
- Do not mix conflicting corner radii that clash with the system's `6px` baseline.
- Do not truncate or drop border contrast on `icon-module_rL-JZG_druids_search-bar_error-icon` fields and secondary buttons.
